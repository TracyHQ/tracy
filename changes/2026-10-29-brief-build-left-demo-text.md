- **A brief build never leaves a copy line in the demo's words, and the leftover check reads every text slot** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** brief builds on a Joomla contract, and the demo-leftover check (kind C09). A line the site plan gives the copy writer that still holds the template's words after the writer (pended, unanswered, refused by the site, dropped by a guard, echoed as a whole sentence, or the demo's sentence with only the company name swapped in) now takes the site plan's fallback, its role's neutral words or the business's own description, else it is emptied after the write. Emptying now also reaches an article's `introtext`/`fulltext`. C09 also reports the demo's words in headings, paragraphs and steps, and a long demo sentence whose name was swapped.
  **Why / Measured:** a local pilot build of a coaching business kept step descriptions and paragraphs of the template's sport on its home page, and "We opened <business> in 2008" on its class pages.
  **Follow-up:** a card's fallback is its own offering's quote, or its label when the quote names another offering. A demo line naming the demo company beside a year, a founding or a tally is never renamed onto the owner; it is emptied (also with no site plan, and for a page description). C09 no longer counts a word that names a page the site serves, or a one-word sample met only in running text.
  **Line limits:** every contract write joins the lines past a slot's measured line count (`fit.lines`) onto its last line, so the site no longer refuses a writer's or fallback's line with "more lines than its box holds"; a refusal naming a line count is written again within it. The writer is told `maxLines` where the demo's own words fit it.
  **Bound nodes:** a records write (emptied fact fields, contact links, social links) never removes or reshapes a node a contract slot binds to: emptied words become a no-break space node for node, a picture is hidden by an attribute, and a step that would still take a bound node away is held. These writes now run after every contract write of the tidy-up, so a later contract write is never refused with "HTML slot is missing or ambiguous".
  **JSON lists and more records:** a card taken off a JSON list is emptied in place instead of spliced out (a spliced list left the last item's path without words, and the plugin's inspect refused every later contract write); the bound-node check covers JSON paths too. Demo lines in a custom module's content, a contact's address lines and its note are now emptied through their own records, and the note about lines that could not be taken off names them.
