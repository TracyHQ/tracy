- **Brief readiness blocks a build when the brief holds nothing of the business: no offering, story or fact (new check `business-content`).** — WO maintainer · issue #554 · `fix/wo-readiness-empty-reading` · 2026-11-11
  **Affects:** Website Opportunities builds and the seller's progress views. A brief with none of these is now `block`: the reading's offerings, an offering fact from any source (`facts[field=services|menu]`), a published menu the Menu page shows from its source, the reading's story or a post's `content.story`, an About fact (the reading's or the logo's), a menu or service line, or a post's dish, product, service, event, offer or news item. A profile bio alone does not count, and neither does a demo request's `business.description` (it arrives as the bio); its typed `business.items` do. The seller's reason says so: it names services, menu items or products and points to Research again. The brief is `block` with `next: research-again` and owner question `owner.business-content`; the action stops `BRIEF_NOT_READY` before any site, chat or model run, and both progress views name the new reason (`brief.check.business-content`, in all eight languages). A business with offerings but no named team is unchanged. Readiness is stored on the revision, so only new revisions carry the check. Deploy tracy-web and the rebuilt Website Opportunities and locale clients together. The first press still reserves its daily build at accept, as for every other blocked check; a Retry of the same brief is refused before usage (#541).
  **Why / Measured:** Portland City Boxing revision 3 (dev, 2026-11-10) had 0 offerings and 0 people, passed every check and built a site with no classes, prices or coaches for about $1.83. Unit, runner (PostgreSQL) and client tests for the empty reading failed before the change and pass after it.
