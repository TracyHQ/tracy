- **Content coverage declares the items lists of category pages reached by URLs without ids and of tag menu items; the template kit declares no items list on an empty category page** — Quickstart tools · `feat/ja-voyara-readiness` · 2026-11-07
  **Affects:** contracts rebuilt with `content-coverage --fit` (`content-coverage/lib/listing-lists.mjs`): a category page no menu item names, reached at the category's path below a category or categories view (Joomla's URLs without ids, `pages/blog/adventure`), now declares `itemLists["category-N.items"]` like the `<id>-<alias>` form did; a `com_tags` menu item of one tag (`view=tag&id[0]=N`) declares `itemLists["<menu item>.items"]` with the tag as source and `display_num` as its page size. Template kits (`template-kit/lib/kit.mjs`): a category page whose category holds no item in the map and shows no item row gets no items list, so it no longer counts in `itemsListsWithoutCardFields`.
  **Why / Measured:** On the JA Voyara 1.0.12 kit 28 items lists had no card fields: 17 sub-category pages reached without ids, 5 tag menu item pages and 3 empty category pages (plus their further result pages). Rebuilt from the same findings, the check reads 0.
