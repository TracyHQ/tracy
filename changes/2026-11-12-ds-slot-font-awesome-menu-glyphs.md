- **Keep Font Awesome menu glyphs transparent in the Joomla design-system module** — template readiness maintainers · #625 · `feat/joomla-t4-batch-1-road-to-live` · 2026-11-12
  **Affects:** the Joomla design-system emitter. T4 puts `.toggle-bars` on Font Awesome glyphs too (JA Vega); those glyphs no longer receive the fill meant for CSS-drawn bars, so the header menu button keeps its three bars. Plain bars and their pseudo-elements keep the fill (Fit, Donate, Stark, Boxing, Tracy Business).
  **Why / Measured:** on a fresh JA Vega 1.0.10 restore at 390 px the filled glyph showed a solid rectangle; removing the fill restored the bars. `test/joomla-menu-bars.test.mjs` failed before and passes after. The pinned default and brand-mode module hashes for JA Boxing and Tracy Business were re-pinned: old and new modules differ only in that one selector (JA Kinetic's module is unchanged). The JA Vega 1.0.10 brand row is not part of this change; it lands with that release's contract.
