- **A copy line is refused only for a business fact the brief does not state; the system checks it against the whole brief, and a build's text spend sums prepare and apply with a dollar estimate.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds with a content brief (Joomla today; the rule in `fact-sources.mjs` is CMS-neutral). Under the approved definition, the system checks each fact against the whole brief fact sheet, not against the writer's citations. A fact the brief states anywhere passes, even when the writer cited no sourceId or a wrong one; the plan's `sources` now come from that lookup plus any valid citations, and `brief.`/`facts.` prefixes on cited ids are accepted. Only business facts count: money, phone, email, web address, street address, dates, years, opening hours, names of people or partners, counts about this business, and awards, certifications, rankings or guarantees. Trade knowledge with numbers ("played 2 vs 2", "24/7", "1-on-1", "a 60-minute session", "Heading 1", "404 page") needs no source. The send-back now names each fact the brief does not state and asks for the line without it; after one send-back the line is still refused. In a replay of the R14 page writer, first answers flagged 38 lines before this change and 9 after; the 9 left were all invented facts: prices, emails and bare social links. The site direction now also reaches pages when the model keys them by route ("home") instead of by page id. In R14 every page entry had been dropped this way. Text spend: `withTextSpend` keys a build by its `time` recorder, so apply adds to prepare's totals instead of replacing them. Before this, R14 recorded 3 calls against 54 real ones. A seed model's `usage()` names its model, and `textCostUsd` is recorded from `quality-prices.json` as an approximation; any model without a price row is listed in `textUnpriced`. Footer label fallbacks reach the receipt (`note`, `footerTextFallbacks`). Debt: the WordPress and EmDash writers do not use the rule yet. Counts written in words ("six courts") are not detected.
