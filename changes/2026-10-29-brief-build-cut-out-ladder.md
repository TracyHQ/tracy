- **Brief build cut-outs: never drawn, taken off by default, framed only where a column would be left empty** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** every brief build that fills a Joomla template with transparent figure slots (Slot fit kind `cutout`); the host gains `POST /_host/picture-overlap`, called once after each Joomla brief build; the Joomla engine takes a `pictureOverlap` dependency.
  - A cut-out slot climbs a ladder (`domain/cutout-ladder.mjs`): the owner's own clear-ground picture of the section's subject (all four corners transparent), stood in the demo figure's place and never covering more of the frame than the figure did; else taken off (the default): a clear picture, hidden by the head block, so the section's own ground carries it; else, only when the figure stood in its own column beside the words (measured box at most 62% of the desktop width, at least 360 x 240, no backdrop picture in the section), the photo the fill plan chose for the slot, in a frame with rounded corners and a soft shadow on a clear ground.
  - The picture model is no longer asked for cut-out objects and no ground is removed from any drawing.
  - The head block takes a `pictures` list: those images are hidden wherever they are shown, and the owner picture that repaints demo backgrounds is never one of them.
  - Safety net: after the head block is written, `POST /_host/picture-overlap` (tracy-host `picture-overlap-browser.js`) reads the home page and up to four kept pages at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390; a build picture (under `tracy-content/`) that covers a heading, a paragraph, a button or the logo is added to the head block's hidden pictures (head block written again, records untouched: `writeOutsideMap` `headOnly`) and named in the build notes with its widths and words. It never fails the build or the QA gate; no host or a failed check leaves a note.
  - A transparent demo PNG the content map never measured (no Slot fit) is shown to the photo labeller on a plain ground (at most 8 a build, largest first; `domain/unmeasured-figures.mjs`). A person, an object, a product, an animal or food: it climbs the ladder (step 1 own clear picture, else step 2 taken off; never framed, its zone is unknown). A shape, line, pattern, blob or texture, or no labeller answer: it stays.
  **Why / Measured:** a local brief build drew a ball on a white ground for a hero figure, cut it free with a jagged fringe and scaled it to the figure's box, so it covered the headline and the button; a second figure, cleared, left half of its section empty.
