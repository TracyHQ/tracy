- **Brief builds take off a section card whose title the writer left in the demo's words, and name menu entries after the category, home or alias page they open.** — Akemi (agent) · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** On a Joomla brief build, a title of a repeated card (step, FAQ question, event, package, claim or card heading, an offering name the site plan does not give) is never trusted when it echoes the demo; with no fallback from the site plan, a card of a section's JSON list is emptied in place by the records write, and a section none of whose cards keeps its own title is unpublished. A card that is a page of its own keeps a neutral title. A demo menu label now takes the title of the category, home page or alias target it opens before falling back to "Learn more". The writer's `shows` hint no longer names the kind of cards the site plan hides.
