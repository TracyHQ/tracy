- **A WO brief whose picture labels are absent, failed, pending, partial or made with no vision model is recovered by the next Refresh or build, without Research again, and a good brief is reused before anything is labelled** — WO maintainer · #561 (issues #555, #556) · `fix/wo-brief-labels` · 2026-11-11
  **Affects:** Website Opportunities briefs (`apps/tracy-web/src/opportunities-build/revisions.js`, `brief-picture-labels.js`, `media-analysis.js`, `build-store.js revisionsOf`, `route.js briefStatus`; the brief's `- Labels:` line in `packages/wo/website-opportunities/domain/brief.mjs`).
  - A brief made with no labelling step records `assetLabelling` `failed / not_attempted`. A labelling that finds its photographs claimed by another request records `pending: <n>` and `reason: labels_pending` instead of `failed / no_bytes`.
  - A brief's labels are incomplete when its `labelled-share` readiness check is short of `pass` with photos and its labelling is absent, `failed`, `off`, `partial` or has `pending` pictures (`labelsIncomplete`). Such a brief is never reused and never changed. The Details page shows it as needing Refresh, and Refresh writes a new revision once labels exist.
  - `researchKey` is unchanged, so complete briefs keep their stored hashes. An incomplete brief is stored under a derived "incomplete" key. A labelled brief whose key an older incomplete brief holds is stored under a derived "relabelled" key (`researchKeySlots`).
  - A complete brief of the same research is looked up before the labeller runs.
  - A picture the vision model was shown and gave no label for is not asked again by a brief. For a place's picture, `wo_media_analysis` keeps it as `failed` with `{unanswered: <reason>}`, and a build may still claim it. For an opportunity's own picture, its `wo_media.labels` keeps `visionUnanswered: {key, model, reason, at}`; it is read from the row, so a demo action's frozen read sees it, it is left out of `researchKey` like `vision`, and a later label removes it. `http_error` and `timeout` are not kept.
  - A demo action labelling beside the request (`pictureLabelRuns`) adopts a complete brief of the same research before asking for any labelling, as a Refresh does.
  - Expect stored briefs whose readiness is short of `pass` on labels, and briefs made where no vision model is assigned, to show Refresh on the Details page. Tests that make a brief with no labeller and expect it current must pass one, as every production Refresh and build does.
  **Why / Measured:** dev, develop f25af7cb7, 2026-11-10: revision 1 of Portland City Boxing had no `assetLabelling` (the server build before 519097e1f passed no labeller), and revision 2 recorded `failed / no_bytes` after a cut-off Refresh. Both stayed blocked on `labelled-share` until Research again. `apps/tracy-web/test/wo-brief-picture-labels-recovery.test.mjs` covers both cases, the label-reuse lookup, the no-repeat rule for unanswered pictures and the partial/off states.
