- **The residue sweep sends a page's words as one `content.replace` call, so each record is saved once per round instead of once per (page, words)** — Hano · #25 · `fix/wo-sweep-one-write-per-page` · 2026-10-28
  **Affects:** agent demo checks on every CMS (`apps/tracy-web` `residue-sweep.js` `planSweep`, dsh `tracy-site-tools` `demo-lifecycle.mjs`, `replace.mjs`). Takes effect once both tracy-web and the `tracy-dsh` image are deployed. A dsh with this change sends `sweepBatches: true` on `check.start`/`check`, and only then does tracy-web group a page's pairs into one call with `changes:[{find, replace, expect}]`. Either side on its own keeps the old one-pair-per-call sweep. `content.replace` now accepts `expect` on each `changes[]` item: that pair is counted on its own (COUNT_MISMATCH names it with `change`), and confirm-count weighs only the places no `expect` counts. Calls without per-change `expect` behave exactly as before. The job still writes a call whole or not at all. dsh sets a refused pair aside, reports it as that pair's own refusal and sends the rest again. A pair that overlaps another pair is sent alone afterwards. A refusal that names no pair (TOO_MANY, mixed stored/theme-printed words) sends each pair alone, as before. A call is split and sent again only when its answer proves nothing was written (`refusedBeforeWrite` in `replace.mjs`: a pre-write refusal code with no `written`, `reverted`, `partial`, `undo` or `apply_id`). A lost receipt, a timeout, a thrown call, a 5xx or `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` is reported as each pair's answer and never resent, and a pair waiting behind it is reported `SWEEP_NOT_SENT`. Copy apply (`copy-apply.mjs`) now follows the same rule. Such a call's pairs are listed under a new `unknown` field per page (with the answer's code), never under `notPlaced`, which keeps only pairs proven unwritten. Its `next` then says to read the page with `page.text` first and write only words still shown as they were. Each of `notPlaced`, `unknown` and `issues` still lists at most 30 entries per page. Those cut are counted under `notListed`, and `next` then claims nothing about them being on the site and asks for a `page.text` read. Sweep rows of that kind carry `unknown: true`. A call carries at most 20 pairs, 40 counted places and 5 uncounted places. A shorter phrase that a longer pair of the same page contains, and the next part of a long text, go in a later call. `SWEEP_MAX_CALLS` (120) and `left` still count pairs, so a round takes the same share of each lane and `SWEEP_ROUNDS_MAX` still means the same thing. The sweep's stop note now says "changes still planned".
  **Why / Measured:** dev deployment, 27/10/2026, WordPress ja-diner agent demo: one `tracy_demo check` wrote 362 post updates over 65 posts. The four colour copies of Home (posts 85-88, kept and adapted by the 🔒 showcase rule) took 58 `content.update` rows each, 1-2 s apart, Home 16 and About 17, because each (page, words) pair was its own read-and-save. In tests, five pages with the same three words now plan five calls (one per page) instead of fifteen, and a two-pair call saves its record once on WordPress, Joomla and EmDash (`replace-shared.test.mjs`).
