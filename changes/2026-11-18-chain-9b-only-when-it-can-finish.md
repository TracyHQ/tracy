- **A website opportunity chain build runs step 9b only when it could leave the site clean: a first site check holding a fault 9b never sends hands the site over without the pass** — David (WO pipeline) · #696 · `perf/wo-lat13-chain-tail` · 2026-11-18
  **Affects:** Rebuild and deploy tracy-web. Chain builds: when the first site check holds a blocking fault of a kind the repair pass never sends (`repair-kinds.js` `NOT_SENT_GROUPS`, `NEVER_SENT`, an unknown kind or no page), the site is handed over with that check and `repair-pass.json` says `not-run` with the groups that held it; the build's verdict is the same (the site still needs review). A first check of faults 9b sends only still gets the pass. The write step bench keeps 9b on every run. ADR 0044 decision 30 amended.
  **Why / Measured:** Dev, 17/11/2026: the last three chain builds' 9b passes removed 0, 1 and 0 of 46 to 115 blocking faults in 2.5 to 3.5 minutes each, every first check holding faults 9b never sends; arabian-tea-lat13 took 17.4 minutes, 3.1 of them in that pass (about 14.3 without it).
