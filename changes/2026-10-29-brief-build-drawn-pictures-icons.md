- **A brief build draws the pictures no owner photo fits, and its icons and cut-outs follow the business** — Akemi (Claude) · #194, #195 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** brief builds on a Joomla contract (the Customize engine's picture phase, `writeContractPictures`). A picture slot no brief photo fits is drawn from the owner's photos (outpaint first, then a scene; prompt from the brief's trade and the slot's offering, no text, logos or faces, checked by the photo labeller and redrawn once), reused across pages, capped at 20 per build; with no photo to draw from it takes a brand colour block. Cards that share one demo file get one picture each (`planFill({perSlot: true})`). Item icons are rendered from Tracy's own icon set by the meaning of each item, cut-out figures become an object drawn on a clear ground or are cleared. The demo-leftover check reads pictures in backgrounds, data attributes and resized copies (C01, C04, C05, C06). A drawing the labeller could not check (asked twice) is refused (`labeller-unavailable`) and the slot takes the colour block. A brief with no usable photo still runs this plan, so no demo picture stays. Each kept card's picture follows the site plan's card label (`honesty.cardNames`, #192). The hand-off lists each drawn picture with "Drawn by AI, replace with a real photo." Other writers keep one decision per demo file.
