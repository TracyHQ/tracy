- **A brief build writes pictures and lines the site's Slot fit check accepts** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** brief builds on a Joomla contract (the Customize engine). Every picture is enlarged to its slot's `fit.minWidth` before it is uploaded (`picture-min-width.js`), keeping its shape and transparency; every text line is planned within its measured box (`fit.maxCharacters`, `slot-box-limits.js`), not only its contract limit. A drawing never extends or copies the brief's own logo, or a picture its labeller called no photograph.
  **Why / Measured:** on the local stand a pilot brief build kept nearly every demo picture and its hero heading: the plugin refused each owner photo, drawing and colour block as narrower than `fit.minWidth` (a social post is 360 to 1080 pixels; the slots ask 672 to 2160) and each line over its box, and the hero drawing was an outpaint of the logo, refused for its text.
