- **A brief build writes pictures and lines the site's Slot fit check accepts** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** brief builds on a Joomla contract (the Customize engine). Every picture is shaped to its slot's Slot fit before it is uploaded (`picture-slot-fit.js`): cropped to the slot box aspect around its focus (or padded clear for a contained picture), enlarged to `fit.minWidth`, and given clear round corners when the slot shows a cut-out and the picture is opaque; every text line is planned within its measured box (`fit.maxCharacters`, `slot-box-limits.js`), not only its contract limit. A drawing never extends or copies the brief's own logo, or a picture its labeller called no photograph.
  **Why / Measured:** on the local stand a pilot brief build kept nearly every demo picture and its hero heading: the plugin refused each owner photo, drawing and colour block as narrower than `fit.minWidth` (a social post is 360 to 1080 pixels; the slots ask 672 to 2160) and each line over its box, and the hero drawing was an outpaint of the logo, refused for its text. Once wide enough, the hero and call-to-action drawings were still refused: they were drawn in the demo file shape (2:1), the plugin holds the slot to its desktop box (1.5).
