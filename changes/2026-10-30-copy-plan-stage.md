- **The copy plan never loses a line a model answer wrote, and its budget is sized by its work** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds on every template (Joomla engine today). A retry of the copy writer is merged key by key: a send-back replaces only the keys it was sent for, and a send-back that fails, comes back empty or is cut keeps the first answer (before, its empty answer replaced the batch and every line went pending). The send-backs of every batch go out as one concurrent, budgeted round (`planConcurrently`), and a batch whose call is lost is asked again split by page, then by section. The words get a budget sized by their calls (`copy-plan-budget.js`: base plus waves of calls times the measured per-call p95, under a ceiling) instead of a fixed 90 s after the site is up; the row records calls, failures, calls cut ("deadline"), rounds, the per-call p95 and the pending keys by reason. A demo line whose call was lost is no longer emptied by the tidy step: it is recorded as "unwritten" with its rule, and the final check reports every demo line left empty or unwritten (`copy-lines`, rule `structure-kept`) instead of reading it as clean.
