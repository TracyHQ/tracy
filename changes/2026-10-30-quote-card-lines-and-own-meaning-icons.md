- **A guest quote card holds one sourced review or nothing, and an item icon says only its own meaning** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** every brief site build (Joomla and WordPress write paths), template authors packing a content map, and the JA Boxing 1.0.8 contract. The packing check (`scripts/slot-roles/check.mjs`, rule `quote-line-outside-group`, error) refuses a content map where a text line of a quote card (read from its field path) stands outside the card's quote group or a `quote.*` role has no group; three released maps are counted, not edited. JA Boxing 1.0.8 declares its pull-quote heading `module-183.1` as `quote.text` in `module-183.quote.0`. The build empties such a line of a released map (`quote-card-line`, never back to the copy writer), and a review's words stand once on a card with two `quote.text` slots. Item icons are chosen only from the item's own words or the offering its own card is named for: the page's offering and the n-th offering of the brief no longer stand in, so a row with any item left unmatched shows one neutral mark on every item.
  **Why / Measured:** on a pilot build the club blurb filled the pull-quote heading above a reviewer's name, and a padel page's what-to-bring row showed a swimmer and a barbell taken from other offerings (criteria 2.2 and 3.5).
