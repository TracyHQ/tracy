- **A build never takes off the home page's menu entry or a kept page's, and never hands over a site that stopped answering** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** every Joomla Customize and brief build (the menu tidy-up). No tidy-up step may unpublish the home entry or the entry of a page the site plan keeps, whichever judge names it. After the tidy-up the engine reads the home page and every kept page; when one does not answer 200, the tidy-up's menu take-offs are written back and the report says which page failed and what was taken back.
  **Why / Measured:** a local brief build took the demo blog's menu entries off with its demo posts; the home entry and the kept About page open the same featured view, went with them, and every page of the site answered 404 "Component not found".
