- **Pages are added by copying a template page layout.** — build maintainers · #238 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** Joomla brief builds, template packers and operators reading /ops content coverage. When the brief lists more items than the template has sample pages, the accepted site plan may add a page that copies a record page family (`record-page:<id>`) or a page that opens an article of its own; the plan check refuses a copy of a list page or of nothing, and an added page that shows no brief item, an item another added page shows, or one a template record already shows (never more pages than the brief has items). The plan's author writes each added page in the writing order, before the pages that list its item, and its words are kept apart from the record it copies, with no demo line left. At Apply the build copies the template record into a new article of the same category (the listing page and the footer's offerings column list it), or copies the menu entry and its "only on these pages" blocks for a page copy, and links every button the plan sends to an added page. Content maps name the page that lists each record page family (`recordPages[].listing`); new readiness rule `record-page-listing` (warning, /ops content coverage); JA Boxing 1.0.8 declares it. Pictures of an added page follow the template record it copies for now. WordPress and EmDash have no writer for added pages yet. Rebuild the web image before the next brief build.
