- **The build chat ends a stopped build draft with a stop card: why, Try again and Start over (#575).** -- AI quickstart work for #575 | PR pending | `feat/ai-quickstart-575` | 2026-11-17
  **Affects:** owners of a build chat whose step stopped or whose start was refused for its hourly or daily limit. Instead of one sentence, the chat ends with a card (the "Could not finish" notice of the approved mock): one plain sentence, for a limit the local time the owner may try again, Try again (disabled until then; absent when the step may not run again or after two retries that stopped again) and Start over (asks once, then calls the existing `POST /api/build-draft/start-over` from the browser with the chat's session and opens the new conversation). The card is published on the session's last turn only, so older turns keep no live buttons; a chat opened again reads `GET /api/build-draft/engine` once, so the card follows the current state (none while a step runs, and the card of a stop the chat never heard). Engine: a `research_limit` stop now carries `rerun: true`, and research asking for the business details says `reason: 'details-asked'`. `tracy_build`: stopped answers carry the engine's `reason`; a start refused for its limit (`paused`) carries `rerun: true` and `again`, and "Please try again." after it starts that call again. `@tracy/core/build-chat` gains `stopCardOf`; the fold's stop carries `reason`, `retryAt` and `paused`. The old Try again tail (`TryAgain.tsx`) is replaced by the card. Eight new strings in every interface language; `@tracy/dsh-build-chat` and both locale bundles rebuilt.
