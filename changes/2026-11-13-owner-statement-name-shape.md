- **The owner's chat statement: a sentence typed into a name field is prose, held names keep their joined forms, and the plan's withheld ids read encoded names (D46 third review fixes).** -- AI quickstart work for #575 (#609) | PR pending | `feat/ai-quickstart-575` | 2026-11-13
  **Affects:** Whoever reads a business reading or a brief made with an owner statement, every caller of the shared held name matcher, and the plan's withheld ids. A name or value field (a service, a price, an audience, a person, a role, a place) is exempt from the echo guard only while it has a name's shape (`owner-name-shape.mjs`: a word limit per field for owner items, a run of seven owner words or most of an owner sentence for every item, and a cut, two sentence or clause signal); a field that fails is recorded with `why: 'owner_prose_in_name_field'` and its fact moves to `ownerFacts`, cut back to whole words. A seven word service name taken whole from the statement now moves to `ownerFacts` too. A held name with a part joined by an apostrophe or a hyphen is found written together or apart (`Siobhan ONeill`, `JeanLuc Marchand`), and `heldNamesIn` holds a several word name written as one whole token. `withheldIdsOf` reads every plain form of the write guard (`O&amp;#39;Neill`, `Siobhan%20O%27Neill`). New exports: `heldPersonFormsOf` (`held-person-forms.mjs`) for callers that match plain texts, and `echoProblemsIn` (`owner-echo.mjs`) for a check of the page writer's output. Without a statement and without `withheld`, the reading's request bytes are unchanged.
