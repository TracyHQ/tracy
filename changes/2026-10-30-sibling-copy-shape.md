- **The cards of one list share one text shape: the demo's length and line count for every sibling, no exclamation mark, one spelling per site.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds and template releases. Sibling body slots of one role in one list (numbered groups of a collection, or the same group of sibling entities such as a blog list of offering posts; a list of 150 items is judged the same way) get one band from the template's demo text: its sentences, its length, and the lines the demo takes per measured viewport, the same line count for every sibling (`sibling-copy-shape.mjs`). On Joomla the text model brings every sibling out of the band, empty, with an exclamation mark, or (item lists) about something other than its own title into the band after every other writer (`sibling-copy-writer.js`): owner words are rewritten keeping every figure and name and adding no hard fact, editorial words are free, a refused line is asked once more; nothing is ever shortened or emptied to match a sibling, and a line still out of the band is reported as an engine note. Bodies take the spelling of their own title. Lists of pages (named by menu labels) and page intros are left alone. A new final check (`sibling-copy-check.js`) reports a card row mixing empty and written bodies, sentence counts more than one apart, lengths more than 1.6 times apart, an exclamation mark, a body off its own title, one record shaped differently in two lists (blocker `sibling-copy-shape`), and a word spelt two ways on the site (major). New packing rule `repeated-item-ungrouped` (error): each card of a repeated field list is one numbered group with an `editorial.paragraph` body; JA Boxing 1.0.8 declares its "what to bring" item cards that way. WordPress and EmDash do not yet run the sibling writer. Rebuild the web image before the next brief build.
