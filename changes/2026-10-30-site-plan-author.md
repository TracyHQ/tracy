- **One author writes a site plan before the copy writers, and every brief photo gets a decision.** — build maintainers · #234 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** Joomla brief builds and operators reading the build record. Before the copy writers, one call on the `woEditorial` door with reasoning on (the text door when editorial is unassigned) reads the template inventory, the brief (business fields, fact sheet, brief photos with their labels) and the rulebook `site-plan-rulebook.md`, and answers a `site-plan/1` plan. The plan check refuses it with every reason and the same author revises it, at most three rounds; a plan still refused, or a failed call, never fails the build. The run (plan, rounds, reasons, tokens, seconds and dollar estimate per call) is kept on the build record as `sitePlan`, and its spend counts in the build's text spend (stage `site-plan`). The plan does not drive the writers yet (#235). New plan check rules: `photo-undecided`, `photo-decided-twice`, `unknown-photo`, `photo-unusable-used` (a photo whose labels refuse it: a graphic, too small, or a copy of another). The seed model takes `reasoning: true`. WordPress and EmDash brief builds do not run the plan yet. Rebuild the web image before the next brief build.
