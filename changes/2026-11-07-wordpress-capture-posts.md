- **WordPress capture of published posts** - Akemi (Claude) · #452 · `feat/wp-capture-posts` · 2026-11-07
  **Affects:** whoever captures a WordPress contract, and the content map lists check of the readiness verdict (#457). `capture-contract.mjs --posts` (and `batch-contracts.mjs capture --posts`) makes every published post whose type and slug are one row an entity (`kind: "post"`, identity `{ postType, slug }`, key `post-<slug>`) with a slot for its title (`target.field: "post_title"`), its excerpt (`target.field: "post_excerpt"`) when plain, and its content blocks by the page rule; `capture-image-slots.mjs` adds the featured picture of every post entity (`target.meta: "_thumbnail_id"`). Off by default: the default capture is unchanged byte for byte, and a profile carrying post fields binds only on a Claude Cowork plugin that reads them. The lists check no longer counts a synced pattern, template, template part, navigation or global styles entity as a post.
  **Why / Measured:** the JA Diner WP7 map carried none of its 22 published posts, so the check could never pass. A capture with `--posts` of the restored demo makes 22 post entities and 65 slots (22 titles, 22 excerpts, 21 featured pictures), the lock check passes, and the check finds nothing missing. Tests: `capture-contract.test.mjs`, `capture-image-slots.test.mjs`, `template-readiness-wordpress-hidden-lists.test.mjs`.
