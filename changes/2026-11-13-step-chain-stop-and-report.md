- **A step chain build re-runs a failing step once, then stops and reports; an empty model door stops it before any model work; every run keeps its step records, and a build has no site check or repair pass.** -- AI quickstart work for #575 (#591) | PR pending | `feat/ai-quickstart-575` | 2026-11-13
  **Affects:** Step chain builds (`sites/brief-engines/step-chain.js`, `step-chain-steps.js`, ADR 0047). A chain build no longer ends `applied` with a soft failure: a step that fails twice (or is stopped by the deadline) makes the row end `failed`, its error the customer sentence `STEP_CHAIN_STOPPED_MESSAGE`, and `timings.chain` ends `stopped` with `failedStep` and `stop` (step, attempts, inputs, output, error). Readers of `timings.chain.ending` must accept `stopped` (`/api/ops/wo/chain-builds` shows it as `failed`). Stand-in files and the "best version used" outcome are gone from the chain path; the Write step runs the write step alone, without the site check or the 9b repair agent. `STEP_CHAIN_KEEP_FILES` is removed: run folders and their `step-records.json` always stay under `TRACY_STEP_CHAIN_RECORDS_DIR` (default in the system's temporary directory), so a deployment that wants records to survive a restart points it at a volume. The doors `brandAgent`, `brandReviewer`, `sitePlanner`, `siteWriter`, `picturePicker`, `pictureLooker` and `pictureDrawer` must be set in /ops for a chain build to start. Rebuild the tracy-web image to ship.
