- **Brief build: the name its own marks agree on, only its real social networks, and its own mark as the favicon** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** every brief made after this change, and Joomla brief builds (T4 and T3). A listing name that only describes the trade no longer holds the business name off the site; social blocks lose the demo networks without breaking the contract; the browser tab shows the business's mark.
  - Name: a listing name that is only a trade description (category words plus the business's own place, no proper noun, for example "Padel & Squash Coach") is no name and causes no conflict. When the business's own marks (the text on its logo, its social page's display name and handle) agree on one name, that name is the business name, cited by those marks, and every place a site name appears takes it. Two own marks that disagree, or a listing name that is a proper name, still hold the name for the owner. `domain/trade-description-name.mjs`, decided in `place-profile-from-research.mjs`.
  - Socials: a JA ACM social list keeps its length (the contract binds every item by index, so a shorter list was held whole by the bound-node guard and the demo icons stayed with empty links). The business's networks move to the front with their real addresses, then WhatsApp when the brief holds it as a contact channel, drawn with the template's own icon font class; the rest are emptied in place and the empty-frame reader hides them. Custom HTML social lists hide unused links in place (`hidden`) instead of cutting them out. A block left with no network goes. The site plan's `socials` now carries `whatsapp`.
  - Favicon: with no favicon uploaded, a brief build makes one from its logo (the picture of the mark, its words cut away, on the logo's own tile colour), else from the symbol drawn for a business with no logo; it goes through the existing favicon writer. `apps/tracy-web/src/sites/brief-engines/brand-favicon.js` also makes the full set (ICO 16/32/48, 180 apple touch, 192 and 512 PNG); only the 512 PNG is written to the site so far.
