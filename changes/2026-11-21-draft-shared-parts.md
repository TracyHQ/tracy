- **The chain's draft copy can split a heavy "shared" into shared block parts, behind the staff switch `chainTrials.splitShared`** — David (WO pipeline) · #724 · `perf/wo-draft-shared-part` · 2026-11-21
  **Affects:** Rebuild and deploy tracy-web. Nothing changes until staff turn the switch on (`PUT /api/ops/wo/settings` `{"chainTrials": {"splitShared": true}}`): the chain's copy steps then add `--split-shared`, a writer bench run setting frozen with the run (`harness.splitShared`, only with `--parallel-pages`). A "shared" unit heavier than the average part then gives up its heaviest shared blocks as `shared:<block id>` parts; each part's plan, notes, visual instruction and message hold only its own shared blocks. A run without the setting is cut and sent exactly as before.
  **Why / Measured:** #723's door `parts` on dev (21/11/2026): "shared" led every draft copy measured. At Arabian Tea House it took 247 to 420 s alone while the other parts took 31 to 225 s; at BXR London, with the home page, it took 278 to 329 s. This is why eight parts were no faster than five (lat20). Case REG-PR-724-shared-units.
