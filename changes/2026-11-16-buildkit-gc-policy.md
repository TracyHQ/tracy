  **Affects:** both image workflows: the builder step is now `uses: ./.github/actions/buildx-builder` (config `buildkitd.toml` next to it). The first image job on each runner after merge recreates that runner's builder once (`::notice` "Image builder replaced"), and about a minute later GC trims its cache from ~98 GB to ≤ 50 GB. Editing `buildkitd.toml` recreates every builder again on its next job. RAM is not capped (see the comment in `buildkitd.toml`).
  **Why / Measured:** 16/11 on the dev host one builder held 98.5 GB / 3,300 records (disk 78 %): BuildKit v0.33 defaults cap at min(80 %, 100 GB) and 20 % free, so nothing ever fired; records used in the last day = 30.6 GB.
