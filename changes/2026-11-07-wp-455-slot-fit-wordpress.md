- **The content coverage audit measures Slot fit on WordPress demos, and the Slot fit group of Content coverage judges WordPress releases like Joomla** — Template readiness (spec #452, ticket #455) · `feat/wp-455-slot-fit-wordpress` · 2026-11-07
  **Affects:** `apps/tracy-web/src/lib/content-coverage/policy.json` now lists `wordpress` in `fitPlatforms`: a WordPress release not shown on Design inspiration whose content map has no Slot fit, a missing fit or a broken one is held from Publish (`coverage-fit`), a shown one warns (`coverage-live-fit`). Every released WordPress contract declares no Slot fit, so each new WordPress release now needs `cli.mjs --fit --write` with its next contract version. In `content-coverage/lib/slot-fit.mjs` a WordPress picture's kind reads its block name (`metadata.name`), not the page slug of its label; Joomla maps carry no page entity and no `target.block`, so Joomla fit is unchanged.
  **Why / Measured:** On the JA Diner WP7 1.0.1 demo the audit with `--fit` gave a fit to all 342 slots (83 pictures measured: 72 photos, 11 cut-outs; 257 of 259 texts measured, the 2 left are the site name and the empty tagline, not shown) and the map passes `checkContentMapFit`.
