- **Template kit: every list row says which card it prints in and which text slots print beside its picture, and a new self-check `rowPartsUnpaired` holds a kit that cannot tell** · Claude, for Akemi · #PR · `feat/kit-row-pairs` · 2026-11-05
  **Affects:** whoever builds or settles a template kit, and the site planner that reads it. The frames pass now reads, at the first width and before any frame is emptied, the repeated card each list row's picture sits in (`lib/read-row-cards.js`, `rowCard` in `frame-evidence.json`). `template-kit.json` carries `lists[].rows[i].printsIn` (the card's signature, or `block`) and `lists[].rows[i].pairedSlots` (text slot ids printed in that card), with `lists[].rowPairsFrom: "measured"` (or `"declared"` for a hand-written kit). `checks.rowPartsUnpaired` counts a list that mixes a lone row with card rows where a card row pairs nothing; /ops names it "card rows without paired texts". Kits built before this change carry no `printsIn` and count 0 until measured again.
  **Why / Measured:** on the JA Boxing class pages, a picture list holds an intro clip and four exercise cards whose heading and ticked lines are plain block slots; the planner kept the four text cards and cut the list to two rows, leaving three cards without a picture, and nothing in the kit said the texts and pictures go together. Measured on a fabricated class page in Playwright (`test/template-kit-row-pairs.test.mjs`); the real kit is measured on a later quickstart version.
