- **A sync loads the live reads beside the copy's dump, and a machine runs two heavy webroot jobs at once** — David (Claude) · #14 · `perf/live-sync-latency-3` · 2026-10-27
  **Affects:** owners of imported sites pressing Sync from live, Preview, Send or Undo, and anyone whose demo installs or backups queue on a busy host. `webroot-sync.sh merge` starts loading the base and the live read into their scratch schemas (`load_reads`, in the background) while it dumps the copy, instead of after (each took 9–11 s on a WordPress site, one after the other); every early return waits for those loads before the scratch schemas are dropped. The machine-wide heavy lock (`/var/lock/tracy-webroot-heavy.lock`) is now `TRACY_HEAVY_SLOTS` slots (default 2; `1` is the old one-at-a-time), shared by `webroot-sync.sh`, `webroot-live-push.sh`, `webroot-backup.sh`, `webroot-git.sh` and `webroot-pack.sh` through `lib/heavy-slots.sh`: a measured preview spent 20–56 s waiting for another site's sync. Slot 0 is the old lock file, so an image without this change still excludes a job holding it.
  **Deploy debt:** the fleet scripts ship in the `tracy-host` image, rebuilt on merge to `develop`; production with the next `develop` to `main` release. Set `TRACY_HEAVY_SLOTS=1` in the host's environment to keep one heavy job at a time on a small machine.
