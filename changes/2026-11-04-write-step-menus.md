- **Write step bench: article pages get their title, description and share picture, footer links to them stop dying, and a site share image prints** · Claude, for Akemi · `feat/write-step-menus` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** whoever runs the write step bench or reads its report. A page that is an article routed under a category's menu item now gets `attribs.article_page_title`, `metadesc` and `attribs.og_img` on the article, not a skipped line. The share stage no longer writes `system_opengraph` on template styles; its `share-switch:*` lines are gone and a `site:share-image` line (`template.siteSettings`, `other_shareImage`) replaces them. A link to an article page is written as a path, without the host the door was reached at.
  **Why / Measured:** the four service pages were reported as "no published menu item opens this page". Their menu rows were never unpublished: the pages have no menu item of their own, and they answer 200. Links to them were dead for another reason: `content.list` names an article's `url` with the container host, and the link stage wrote it into the footer menu items. No `og:image` printed because T4 reads the switch for content and contact pages from the file `etc/global.json`, not from the style; putting the switch in that file by hand printed `og:image` on all nine kept pages. That file is locked by the contract and no door writes it, so a page's own picture still needs a plugin change.
