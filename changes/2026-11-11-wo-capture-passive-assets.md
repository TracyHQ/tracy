- **Keep template capture from stalling on passive external assets while preserving their real bytes.** — WO pipeline maintainer · #558 · `fix/wo-diner-section-capture` · 2026-11-11
  **Affects:** Template kit preparers. Tool 3.1.2 uses bounded Playwright request transport for external images, fonts and stylesheets at their original URLs, preserving status, headers and browser-followed redirects. Same-site and active requests retain their existing paths. Responses are disposed, and failures or oversized fulfilled bodies fall back to the browser.
  **Why / Measured:** A fresh Diner capture spent 908 seconds reading 51 pages at two widths because Google font stylesheets and the T3 attribution image stalled in Chromium. Original-URL transport rendered the About page with the actual fonts in 4.2 seconds at desktop and 4.7 seconds on phone; downloaded font hashes and section dimensions matched the original resource evidence. Released kits remain immutable and require a new reviewed capture before use.
