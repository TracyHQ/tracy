- **Allow an explicit full-chain Retry after a terminal failed write recorded as applied.** — pipeline maintainers · #541 · `fix/wo-artifact-integration` · 2026-11-11
  **Affects:** WO action acceptance and existing-site chain restart; rebuild the web image. A terminal `applied` brief is eligible only when the chain ended in `soft-failure`, no steps remain running and its latest write failed. Healthy or incomplete measurements stay ineligible. Acceptance and restart retain exact account, site generation, frozen revision, ownership, live-writer, live-turn, QA lease and snapshot-hash fences. The application reads this eligibility only with server-held retry authority; ordinary failed-brief retry behavior stays unchanged. A retry prepares a fresh chain on the same site instead of resuming old checker outputs, preserving historical actions and bench outputs.
