- **Write step bench, part 2: a bench-only command writes a frozen site copy to a trial site and produces the write report** · Claude, for Akemi · #354 · `feat/write-step-bench` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** whoever runs the benches on dev, and anyone reading refusals from `applyJoomlaContract`. New commands `run`, `list`, `status <run>`, `get <run> --out <dir>` and `cancel <run>` in `scripts/write-step-bench.mjs`; the site record commands are now `site-status` and `site-list`. New table `write_bench_run` in migration 198. `run` executes in the command's own process and refuses any site the bench did not make itself. Before writing it checks the files and builds the owner table, so a missing or bad file, an unknown slot or a two-owner dispute stops the run with nothing written and the report names the cause. Slots the plugin refuses are dropped and reported in the plugin's words. Hide decisions, link targets and the export's extras are reported as skipped; later tickets write them. In `tracy-seeder`, a thrown refusal from `applyJoomlaContract` now also carries `errors` and `answer`; its sentence and its behaviour for existing callers are unchanged. No customer-facing change.
  **Why / Measured:** the copy, picture and brand files have never reached a site, so no one can judge them in place. The contract door is all or nothing, and its wrapper was dropping the per-slot list needed to write around a refusal.
