- **Every packed 1440 page and block shot of a template kit carries a short text of what it shows (`look`), and /ops holds a kit without them** — Claude, for Akemi · no PR yet · `feat/template-kit-image-looks` · 2026-11-03
  **Affects:** whoever packs a template kit, and the Template kit step on the Road to live. A page or block in `template-kit.json` may carry `look: { image, sha256, text }`; `cli.mjs looks --kit <file> --list` names the shots without a current one (absolute file paths) and `cli.mjs looks --kit <file> --apply <looks.json>` checks and merges the texts written by the packager's own Claude Code (skill `quickstart-contract`, `references/image-looks.md`; no model, network or browser call in the tool). `pack` copies a look to the pages and blocks drawn by another one's shot, and a rebuild keeps the looks of unchanged shots. The step reads `kit-incomplete` with `params.missingLooks` while any packed 1440 shot has no current look, so the committed JA Boxing 1.0.10 kit reads `kit-incomplete` until its looks are written; the approval still covers images only and stays valid.
