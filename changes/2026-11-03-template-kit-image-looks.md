- **Every packed 1440 page and block shot of a template kit carries a short text of what it shows (`look`), and /ops holds a kit without them** — Claude, for Akemi · #313 · `feat/template-kit-image-looks` · 2026-11-03
  **Affects:** whoever packs a template kit, and the Template kit step on the Road to live. A page or block in `template-kit.json` may carry `look: { image, sha256, text }`; `cli.mjs looks --kit <file> --list` names the shots without a current one (absolute file paths) and `cli.mjs looks --kit <file> --apply <looks.json>` checks and merges the texts written by the packager's own Claude Code (skill `quickstart-contract`, `references/image-looks.md`; no model, network or browser call in the tool). `pack` copies a look to the pages and blocks drawn by another one's shot, and a rebuild keeps the looks of unchanged shots. The step reads `kit-incomplete` with `params.missingLooks` while any packed 1440 shot has no current look, the approval still covers images only and stays valid. The committed JA Boxing 1.0.10 kit carries 125 looks (55 distinct 1440 shots) and its release zip was rebuilt with them (same images).
