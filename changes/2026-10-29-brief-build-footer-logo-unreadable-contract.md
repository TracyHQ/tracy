- **A footer logo is placed even when the content contract can no longer read the site** - Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** Joomla brief builds with a footer logo slot. When the contract refuses for a reason that is not about the picture (it cannot read a record an earlier step rewrote, "HTML slot is missing or ambiguous"), the logo, still sized by the slot's fit or the template row's frame, goes into that slot's module HTML in place of the template's logo file. A refusal that judges the picture (a `SLOT_` code) still leaves the business name as text.
  **Why / Measured:** on a local brief build on a Joomla T4 template every contract apply after the tidy-up was refused, because a demo figure emptied outside the contract left one of its text slots with no node; the footer then showed the name instead of the owner's logo.
