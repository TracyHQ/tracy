- **WordPress capture: inline markup slots and theme picture options and meta** · Akemi0x · TracyHQ/tracy#459 (spec #452) · `feat/wp-459-diner-wp7-release` · 2026-11-07
  **Affects:** whoever captures a WordPress contract. `capture-contract.mjs --inline-markup` makes a slot for a named heading, paragraph, list item or button whose element marks words inline (`markup: "inline"`, the sample in the content map notation, the limit counted on the words), only when the Claude Cowork plugin can write that markup back (`inline-markup.mjs`, the plugin's allowlist); `--theme-picture-options` and `--theme-picture-meta` declare `manifest.themePictures` and make an entity of each option, and `capture-image-slots.mjs` makes the image slots of every declared option and post meta key. Both off by default, so released profiles re-capture byte for byte. Claude Cowork 0.18.39 or later binds them (TracyHQ/claude-cowork#286).
  **Why / Measured:** JA Diner WP7 1.0.2 left 186 coverage holes open, about 100 of them with no write door: words marked inline in one block and pictures the theme prints from its own option or post meta. On the 1.0.2 dump the notation matches the plugin's own reader on all 350 marked-up blocks, and 282 of them round-trip through the plugin's writer unchanged; every one of them is an unnamed block, so they need a name in the next cut. The three theme picture keys of the theme are declared and produce their entities.
