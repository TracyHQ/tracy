- **Every step chain build records minutes and USD per step, and /ops/wo shows them per site (#480, #481)**: Website Opportunities · #480 #481 · `feat/wo-build-and-measure` · 2026-11-08
  **Affects:** the run frame (`sites/brief-build.js`), the chain engine (`sites/brief-engines/step-chain.js`), a new staff door `GET /api/ops/wo/chain-builds` (`ops/wo.js`, `lib/wo-chain-builds.js`) and a new "Chain builds" view of `/ops/wo` (`packages/web/tracy-harness`, namespace `wo-builds`). `site_brief.timings.chain` already held minutes and USD for the kit, brand, planner, copy, pictures and write steps (USD from each step's own spend record); it now also holds the base site as a step (minutes from the press until the host had the site up, no USD). The door lists one row per chain-built site, newest first: site link, template, start, total minutes, total USD, ending (complete, or soft failure at a named step) and per-step minutes and USD. A build longer than twice the median of the builds listed is flagged (needs at least three builds). Internal emails only; anyone else gets a bare 403 and nothing is read. No schema change, no public API change.
  **Why / Measured:** nobody knows what a real chain build costs or how long it takes. Covered by `test/ops-wo-chain-builds.test.mjs` (real Postgres read, highlight rule, staff and non-staff door), `test/step-chain-build.test.mjs` (base site step) and `test/wo-address.test.mjs`. The table was rendered with fictional data in light and dark.
  **Also:** the engine keeps a snapshot of the run so far on the row while the chain runs (`timings.chain` with `ending: running`, the steps under way and the spend of finished steps), through a new `ctx.progress` of the run frame. The table lists a running build with its elapsed minutes and current step, and highlights it once past the slow threshold; a row that is no longer open with only a snapshot is shown as a hard failure at the step that was under way.
