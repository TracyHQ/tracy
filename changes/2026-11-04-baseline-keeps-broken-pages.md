- **A page the untouched template itself breaks no longer costs the whole demo baseline, and the kit's page title gate skips pages not for customers** — Claude, for Akemi · #376 · `feat/write-step-bench` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** whoever measures a demo baseline or settles a template kit. `ensureBaseline` keeps a page that answered an error, was not found, or was sent home as `unread` in the baseline file; `demo-words` and `demo-pictures` warn that such a page has no demo to compare. A page the host could not reach, a broken home, or more than a quarter of the pages failing still throws and keeps nothing. `coverageGate` and `slot-roles` no longer demand a title slot of the page of an account the content map does not declare, and list those pages in `coverage.notForCustomers`.
  **Why / Measured:** the baseline was all or nothing, and the host holds whichever page opens a call to a home's rule, so one not-found page in that place threw away the whole measure.
