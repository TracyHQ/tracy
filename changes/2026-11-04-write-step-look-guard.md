- **The template's look is held at the write: a guard refuses any write that could change more than the accent, icons, pictures, words and logo, and the `template-look` and `icon-look` groups and the baseline's element look are gone** — Akemi · #370 · `feat/write-step-bench` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** the write step and its bench, and anyone reading a site check. A run can now stop before its first write with `LOOK_GUARD`, naming the step at fault, as a template whose copy changes a class in its text does. `report.guard.refused` lists what was refused. The host's look read no longer answers `props`, `head` or `styles`, and page measures no longer answer `pictureBoxes`; an older host still works, and what it sends beyond that is ignored. A new baseline holds `over` at the top level; one kept from before still reads `look.over`.
  **Why / Measured:** the owner judged measuring every element's computed look on an untouched site too heavy. The guard's limits are in ADR 0044, decision 10.
