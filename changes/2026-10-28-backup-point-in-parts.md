- **A sync's result point is dumped in parts side by side** — David (Claude) · #17 · `perf/live-sync-ranges` · 2026-10-28
  **Affects:** owners of imported sites pressing Sync from live, Preview or Send, and anyone restoring or downloading a backup point a sync made. `webroot-backup.sh --op create-from` dumps the sync's result schema — a scratch schema nobody writes — in `TRACY_DUMP_STREAMS` (4) mariadb-dumps side by side, its tables shared out by size, then its routines and events alone; each part is a gzip member of the point's dump, checked whole and ending as mariadb-dump ends, and the file loads in one stream as before. One mariadb-dump of a WordPress result took 9–13 s of every sync while the database had a core idle. `lib/dump_tables.py` (the cut a sync's restore loads) reads such a dump part after part. Every other backup (a person's, Before restore, the Initial one) is dumped as before, in one transaction.
  **Deploy debt:** the fleet scripts ship in the `tracy-host` image, rebuilt on merge to `develop`; production with the next `develop` to `main` release.
