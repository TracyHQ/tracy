- **The write step reads and writes its content contract through a site door chosen by the kit's platform; a WordPress, EmDash or platform-less kit now stops before anything is read or sent** — David (WO) · #PR · `refactor/wo-site-doors` · 2026-11-25
  **Affects:** whoever calls `runWriteStep` or `runStepNine`: the input's `kit` now carries `platform` (as `openKit` gives it), and a kit without one stops with `WRITE_STEP_KIT_PLATFORM`; the step chain and the write step bench pass it. `writeContract` (`write-step/contract-write.js`) takes `applyContract` (the door's) instead of `applyJoomlaContract`. Joomla runs send the same calls as before. WordPress and EmDash kits stop with `WRITE_STEP_NOT_ON_PLATFORM` until their doors exist, which is also the write step bench's refusal now (it said `KIT_PLATFORM: ... Joomla sites only`). New: `write-step/site-doors/` (`siteDoorFor`, `platformStop`), the place the WordPress and EmDash write doors go (WO on three CMS, step 1).
