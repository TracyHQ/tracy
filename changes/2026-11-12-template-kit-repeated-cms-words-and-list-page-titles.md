- **Template kit: copies of one CMS word in a block get distinct ids; a menu-parameter title and later list pages settle their title slot** — template readiness maintainers · #625 · `feat/joomla-t4-batch-1-road-to-live` · 2026-11-12
  **Affects:** template-kit site part and page titles (`template-kit/lib/kit-site.mjs`, `lib/page-titles.mjs`) for every kit built from now on. When a block prints the same language string once per card (a "Show more" button in each career card), each copy, already its own `site.fixedTexts` record with its holder, now gets an id told apart by that holder; a single copy keeps the id it had. A page whose h1 the page reading took from a menu-item parameter (`page_title`) is titled by that parameter's slot, and a later page of a list (`?start=20`) takes the title slot settled on its first page when that slot is one of its candidates. Saved and approved kits are unchanged until rebuilt.
  **Why / Measured:** on JA Nova 1.0.12 the kit failed `fixedTextIdsInvalid` 35 (five "Show more", seven "BUDGET" … sharing one id) and `slotCoverage` 5 (`/category-blog?start=9|18`, `/tagged-items` and its `?start=20|40`). Assembling the same JA Nova kit files again: 0 and 0. The two new tests failed before and pass after; the template-kit suites (429 tests) and the `contracts`, `slot-roles`, `templates` gates pass.
