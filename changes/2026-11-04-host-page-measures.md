- **Write step bench, part 10 (host half): the host answers `pageMeasures`** · Claude, for Akemi · #361 · `feat/host-page-measures` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** whoever deploys `tracy-host` and whoever runs the write step bench. `POST /_host/demo-qa` with `measurePages: true` now reads, on every kept page it opens and at both 1440 and 390, the links, the text boxes that overflow, the blocks over two screens high and the pictures drawn out of shape (`demo-qa-page-measures.js`), and answers them as `pageMeasures` (one entry per page and width, the shape documented in `apps/tracy-web/src/site-check/page-measures.js`) plus a `pageMeasuresSummary` with the link counts. Nothing changes for a caller that does not send `measurePages`: no extra reading, no extra field, and the incremental read cache is keyed on the flag. The four site check groups dead-links, text-overflow, tall-blocks and stretched-pictures stop being "unmeasured" once the host is redeployed. Link status (`demo-qa-links.js`) is a real HEAD, then a GET when the HEAD is refused or fails, at most 120 distinct addresses a run, 4 at a time, 5 s each, and nothing starts after 20 s or after the run's own budget plus 10 s. Same-site links are followed through up to 3 same-site redirects. External links are never requested (a host request to an address a page names is a request to any address, and third parties block crawlers): they are left out of `links` and counted as `external` in the summary. Links past the cap or the time are left out and counted as `overCap` and `outOfTime`, never reported dead. The routes and the post read are not measured.
  **Why / Measured:** the site check had no source for these four measures. Unit tests on made-up pages: the link checker on a fake `fetch`, the answer's shape and its absence when not asked, and a real headless Chromium reading an invented page for each measure at both widths. Not measured: the extra time per page and per call on a real site, and whether the host's 15-pages-per-call budget (180 s) still fits with the link phase; read `durationMs` and `pageMeasuresSummary` on a bench run before changing any limit.
