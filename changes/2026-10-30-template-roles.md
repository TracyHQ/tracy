- **Template sections, card links, offering tabs, outlines, labels and text capacity are declared in the content map, and the build follows the declarations.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds and quickstart contract authors. A section that lists records it does not hold declares what it lists (`entity.lists`, a group kind), and its heading is written for that body instead of a figure in its intro (`sectionBodies`, `sectionShows`). Step groups may declare `presentation: "offering-tabs"` (one tab per brief offering, titled with its name or a shorter form of it; extra tabs hidden, `offering-tabs-plan.mjs`) or `"session-outline"` (the page offering's own outline, never the demo trade's rhythm). A slot may declare `labelOf: "<slot key>"`: when the plan empties the value, its label goes too, and the writer keeps a label standing alone. On Joomla a list link that wraps an item's title is resolved by the title only, not by the other lines of the item. The fit capture measures a text box at the room it can grow into inside a shrunk flex item, not at its demo text's width. New content map checks: `section-body-undeclared`, `card-title-link-undeclared`, `offering-rows-short`, `outline-item-unroled` (errors) and `text-box-at-demo-length` (warning); the domain role check validates `lists`, `presentation` and `labelOf`. The ja-boxing j6 1.0.8 contract carries these declarations. WordPress and EmDash writers do not yet read `lists` or `labels`. Rebuild the web image before the next brief build.
