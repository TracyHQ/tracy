- **The route stage renames a kept page reached through its parent item by its article's alias, and frees a slug a url menu item holds (card P)** — Write step · #415 · `fix/write-step-rewrite-renamed-links` · 2026-11-06
  **Affects:** the write step's route stage (`apps/tracy-web/src/write-step/page-route-parent.js`, `page-route-lines.js`, `page-route-stage.js`): a kept page with no menu item of its own whose parent item opens it takes the plan slug as its article's alias through `content.update`, a rerun gives that article its demo alias back first, and a category view there is refused naming the missing door (the Joomla plugin keeps `alias` closed for a category). A slug a sibling url menu item holds is freed first by giving that item `<slug>-link`; a routed sibling holding it is never touched and the page is refused. The renamed-link stage and the site check follow these renames; `page-slug` reads the article's alias for such a page.
  **Why / Measured:** On a bench site five of seven slug renames were refused: four detail pages had no menu item of their own (the parent category item opens them by the article alias), and the about page's slug was held by a url item of a site-map menu.
