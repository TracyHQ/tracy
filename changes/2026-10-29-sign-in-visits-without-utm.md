- **`/login` now counts every signed-out visit, not only visits from a link with `utm_*`: a visit with no source is kept as an empty `login.visited` row, and a visit linked from another site carries that site's host as `referrer`** — Sarah (Claude) · #PR · `feat/sign-in-visits-without-utm` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** usage readers of `ops_signal` (`/ops` and weekly reports). `login.visited` is no longer "UTM visits only": read `data ? 'source'` for those, `data = '{}'` for direct visits (typed address, bookmark, desktop app) and `data ? 'referrer'` for visits linked from another site without a source. Still sent by the page's own script once per tab, capped at 30 an hour per client address, never the address or a path. No Telegram message.
  **Why:** since 26/10 only visits that carried `utm_*` were counted, so the visit-to-sign-in rate of Google, direct and desktop-app visitors could not be read at all.
