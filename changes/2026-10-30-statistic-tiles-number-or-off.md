- **A statistic tile shows an accepted number with its label and source, or it goes** — Akemi (Codex) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds on templates with counter or statistic tiles (Joomla T3/T4 modules, WordPress and EmDash statistic blocks). Every accepted typed statistic (social follower counts, ratings, review counts, founding year) travels as one number, label and source unit to the site plan, the copy writer and the leftover cleanup. The planned values reach the template write even when the copy reader returned nothing, a tile with no fitting number is taken off (only the tile when its section holds photos), and the final check rejects any rendered tile, including an emptied one, whose number does not match an accepted statistic with its label.
