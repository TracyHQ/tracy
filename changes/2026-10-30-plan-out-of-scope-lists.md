- **The site plan's "brief facts not shown" list is made by code.** — operators and graders reading the build record · #241 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** Joomla brief builds. The accepted site plan saved in the build record (`sitePlan`) always carries both out-of-scope lists with a reason per entry: template elements not used (every dropped page and hidden element, already made from the plan) and now brief facts not shown (every record of the brief's fact sheet that no page, glossary entry or card of the plan places, plus the author's own entries; an entry with no reason gets a default one). The grading tool reads both lists with the plan, the plan's pictures and the copy line trace, and its report links each failed criterion to the plan decision or page behind it. No template or content map change. WordPress and EmDash have no plan author yet.
