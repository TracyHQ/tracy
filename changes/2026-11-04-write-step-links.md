- **The write step now writes the links a kept page shows: each plan link gets a real address through the door that holds it, or its line is failed with the reason** · Claude, for Akemi · #371 · `feat/write-step-links` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** the write step bench (`bench-cli`, /ops) and the site check. A new stage (`write-step/link-stage.js`, `link-doors.js`, `link-targets.js`, `link-lines.js`) runs before the hide stage when a plan has links. A page resolves to its menu item or its routed article address, a contact fact to its address; a slot goes in the one contract call, a plain-link menu item takes `link` on its row after the page settings. Each written line carries `ordered` and a read. A link inside words the plan hides, or on a menu item the plan takes off the site, leaves with them.
  **Why / Measured:** every link line was reported skipped. Run offline on a frozen plan and kit with a fake door that answers from the content map: 142 link lines became 41 written in the contract call, 8 on menu items, 26 already opening the plan's page, 51 leaving with what the plan hides, 12 with no address to write (controls and the map button) and 4 failed (a created legal page, and three links no door holds). Nothing on a real site is measured yet: whether Joomla keeps a menu item's `link` as sent, and every address on the page.
