- **Brief builds fill an item card's fact rows (label and value) with the brief's sourced facts under labels that name them.** — Akemi (agent) · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** On a brief build, the rows of a card's fact list (a timetable, duration, price or detail row of an offering card) no longer go blank when the brief holds no timetable: each row takes the next sourced fact that fits (the offering's own price or audience, then who it is for, where the business serves, opening hours, how to book, a line of its story, a Google rating shown on the site), and its label is rewritten to name it. Every card of a list takes the same kinds in the same order, never more rows than the template's; the rows beyond the facts are emptied and the empty-frame net hides them. English labels only for now (other site languages fall back to English).
