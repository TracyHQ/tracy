- **Sync to live never writes over a field the customer changed during Apply or after it.** — David · issue #121 · `fix/push-expected-fields` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** Sync to live on WordPress. Each `content.update` of an Apply now carries `expected`, every field it writes with the value it just read; Undo carries what the push left. The engine compares them on the record read under its write lock right before writing (claude-cowork `fix/wp-expected-fields`), so a title changed in wp-admin between the read and the write is a conflict ("changed on your live site") instead of being overwritten, and Undo keeps it. An engine without `expected` ignores it and writes as before, so the full protection arrives with that plugin release.

  Verification: two new live-write cases (a title changed in the gap during Apply, and during Undo) against an engine that honours `expected`; live-write suite 58/58 with zero skipped. Engine side: claude-cowork WordPress engine tests 3287 passed (the one failure, an unwritable webroot under a root container, fails on main too). The fleet needs the next shared-script image release.
