- **A brief build's logo fits the template's logo frame** - Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** Joomla (T4 and T3) brief builds. A square, round, tall or tiled logo becomes a horizontal lockup: the owner's mark, cut free of its background tile by image processing and never redrawn, beside the business name set as real type (Be Vietnam Pro SemiBold outlines through `fontkit`, no server font), in a light-ground and a dark-ground variant whose colours come from the owner's logo (text at 4.5:1, mark at 3:1). A brief with no logo gets a symbol drawn through the Customize picture door (no letters: the photo labeller refuses any text) beside the name, in the template's own logo colours; nothing drawn, the name alone as a wordmark. A wide logo is kept, a missing variant recoloured when it is flat. A logo Tracy made adds the owner note "Tracy made a temporary logo from your details; you can replace it at any time". Templates whose profile row has no measured frame use the row's logo height (or 56) at a 3.4 shape. WordPress and EmDash take the brief logo as before.
  **Words in the mark:** whether the owner's mark already holds words is asked of the photo labeller, else read from the picture (lines of letters set apart by empty rows or columns). Words that stand apart are cut away and the picture alone goes beside the typeset name; words woven into the picture (a seal's ring) leave the owner's logo alone, no name added, as tall as the frame. The name stands about half its cap height from the mark and is centred on it.
  **Never on its tile:** the mark is always the cut-free shape. Where it does not reach 3:1 on a ground it is toned: its own lightness pattern mapped onto the logo's darker brand colour (else a darker tone of its own hue), so the inner details stay.
  **One style for every made logo:** a transparent picture: the mark (the owner's, cut free; a drawn symbol inside a solid circle of the template's accent, drawn in white), then the business name in bold condensed capitals (Saira Extra Condensed Bold, OFL, shipped with its licence) in the ground's strong text colour, and under it the kind of business the brief already states (its sub-trade's or trade's noun, a restaurant's one cuisine) in small, widely spaced capitals (Be Vietnam Pro SemiBold) in the accent colour. A name that ends in its kind is split instead ("Saigon Iron" over "Boxing Gym"); a brief that states no reliable kind gets no line. The light and dark variants differ in the name colour, and in the descriptor's shade where one shade of the accent cannot reach 4.5:1 on both grounds.
  **Why / Measured:** on a local brief build on a Joomla T4 template the owner's square badge was dropped into a 195 x 56 header frame and set on a white plate on dark spots; it did not read as a logo.
  **Dependency:** `fontkit` 2.0.4 (MIT, pure JavaScript, already in the tree) added to `@tracy/web`; both lockfiles regenerated.
