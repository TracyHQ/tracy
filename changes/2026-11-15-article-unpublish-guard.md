- **The write step's look guard accepts unpublishing a dropped page's template article, so a chain build no longer carries one blocking finding per dropped page** — David (WO pipeline) · #633 · `perf/wo-picture-look-ahead` · 2026-11-15
  **Affects:** Rebuild and deploy tracy-web.
