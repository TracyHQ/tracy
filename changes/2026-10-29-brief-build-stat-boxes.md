- **Brief build stat boxes: a sourced fact, else an owner photo, else gone; lone empty frames hidden; the leftovers block reaches every page** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** every brief build of a template with stat or fact boxes (group kind `stat_item`), and every Joomla T4 brief build (where the leftovers block now goes and what it hides).
  - A stat box (a number in a field of its own, not inside a sentence) takes one sourced fact of the brief (`domain/stat-box-plan.mjs`): Google's rating and review count only when they are on the site, the years running from a founding year the business states ("EST. 2023"), how many offerings or people the brief names (two or more). One fact per box, never the same fact twice in a section, the fact whose subject the demo label names first; a value too long for its box takes its short form or nothing. Never an estimate, never a customer count.
  - No fact left: a box among the photos of a grid is emptied and kept (record `photo`), and the head block shows one owner photo in it, cover-cropped, its words hidden (`empty-frames.js` `photoTiles`, `leftoverHeadBlock` `tiles`), a photo the site already serves that is not in the same grid; no photo left, the box is hidden and the grid closes. A band of boxes (two or more, no photo beside them) takes off the boxes no fact is left for, and goes whole as a section when none is left.
  - Safety net: a LONE emptied frame (no sibling of its shape) is now hidden too, when it sits beside content and holds nested boxes and no link, icon, picture, code or anything the page's scripts name.
  - The leftovers module on T4 goes to the position whose custom module every served page shows (read from the pages), and a block already written in a position some page does not print is moved there.
  **Why / Measured:** on a local brief build the leftovers module sat in the position of a map module that the layout prints only inside the contact page's map dialog, so no rule of it reached the home or class pages: an emptied stat box stayed a dark hole in the home gallery and emptied class fact rows kept their frame and icon.
