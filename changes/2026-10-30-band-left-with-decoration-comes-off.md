- **A band that loses its working parts comes off whole** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds and template releases. When a brief build takes every working part of a layout band off (a heading, a text, a form, a button), the decoration left in it (a logo, an icon) comes off too, so no logo stands alone in an empty strip. Joomla unpublishes the decoration modules of the band (`layout-bands.mjs`, declared per content map in `layoutBands`, a decoration marked `standalone` stays); the empty-frame pass hides any strip still showing only a logo or an icon beside an emptied column, and the final check reports one left as `lone-decoration-band`. The packing check (`scripts/slot-roles/check.mjs`) refuses a contract whose decoration block shares a band with working parts and no band declares it; JA Boxing 1.0.8 declares its footer band.
