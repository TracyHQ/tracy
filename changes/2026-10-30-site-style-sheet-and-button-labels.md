- **One style sheet per site, sibling cards fall back as one unit, and every button label names where it leads.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds. The site plan carries `styleSheet` (`site-style-sheet.mjs`): British or American English read once from the brief's own words (else its country, else British), sentence case, and the phone, currency and clock the brief uses. Every Joomla writer is told it (page pass, offering lines, card bodies, record pages, call-to-action bands), and after the last writer every line is respelt to the site's spelling, names kept (`site-voice.js`). An offering card's fallback line never copies a social post or a review and passes the same voice filter as written copy (no exclamation mark, no Title Case slogan); the cards of one list fall back together: all from the business's words, or all to the writer, and a line the writer leaves on one card is taken off every sibling (`site-plan-honesty.mjs`, `offering-name-only.js`). A page intro line is never planned for two pages (`page-intros.mjs`). A navigation button names its destination page (`pageNameOf`); a contact button leads to the Contact page (or, with none kept, the brief's own channel) and says the action: the main action when it is taken there ("Book a class"), else "Contact us", "Call us", "Email us" or "Message us on WhatsApp" (`declared-button-plan.mjs`, `contact-buttons.mjs`); "How to reach us" is gone. The final check reads every visible text node, menus and buttons included, for a word spelt two ways (`sibling-copy-check.js`). WordPress and EmDash writers do not yet get the style sheet or the respelling. Rebuild the web image before the next brief build.
