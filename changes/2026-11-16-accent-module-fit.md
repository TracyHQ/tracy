- **A template whose accent appears in many rules still gets the brand accent: the accent module fits the module limit** — David (with Claude) · #663 · `fix/accent-module-compact` · 2026-11-16
  **Affects:** Rebuild and deploy tracy-web. Brand step and write step on templates with a large accent family (JA Diner): the module joins one-declaration rules into selector lists where the cascade allows, matches inline-style colours case-insensitively, and drops the markup forms of the rarest shades before it gives up the accent. Anyone reading the look guard: a joined rule is judged per selector (`selectorParts`).
  **Why / Measured:** Dev, 16/11/2026: JA Diner's module was 121114 bytes, over 64000, and every JA Diner build kept the template's accent (one final-check block). Its sheets now give 60422 bytes with a clean look guard. Case REG-PR-663-accent-module-fit.
