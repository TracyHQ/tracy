- **Build on a numbered brief page builds exactly that brief, and refuses with `BRIEF_STALE` when the latest research no longer gives it** — WO maintainer · issue #553 · `fix/wo-build-brief-binding` · 2026-11-11
  **Affects:** sellers on Website Opportunities › brief page, and callers of `POST /api/website-opportunities/:id/demo/build`. The door takes an optional `revisionId` (not with `retryOf`): after the normal template and selection checks, inside the acceptance transaction and before the day's charge, it must be this opportunity's brief of the current research (404 otherwise, 409 `BRIEF_STALE` `{code,next,revisionId}` when stale), and the worker then builds exactly it. "Current" is the matching `research_hash` for a shared brief; for a quality-v1 brief (no hash) it is the evidence the brief froze (`wo_revision.research`) matching the research now, and `GET /:id`'s `brief.current` uses the same rule. A quality-v1 build writes its own brief and answers 409 `BRIEF_NOT_BINDABLE` to a `revisionId`; the brief page is not affected, because its agent-demo/1 builds are accepted as legacy in every quality mode. Without `revisionId` (the list row, bulk, Find) nothing changes. The brief page sends its revision id; when its brief is already known to be out of date it says a build would be refused, disables Build and offers Go to the place; after a `BRIEF_STALE` refusal it says why. New strings `build.briefOutOfDate`, `build.openPlace`, `reason.brief_stale` in every interface language. Deploy tracy-web and the rebuilt tracy-website-opportunities, tracy-locales and tracy-locale-vi clients together.
  **Why / Measured:** On the page of Brief 2 the form built a new Brief 3 because the research had moved on, and a note computed when the page loaded cannot see research that finishes afterwards. The rebuild door's existing revision binding skips template validation, so the public door got its own checked field instead. The new door tests (`website-opportunities-doors.test.mjs`, #553, including a brief written by the quality writer) and the new client tests failed before the change and pass after.
