- **Schedule rows: a one part schedule is never lost and never cut inside a range.** -- AI quickstart review for #615 | PR pending | `feat/ai-quickstart-575` | 2026-11-13
  **Affects:** The plan rules and the page writer for a list with a days field and a times field. A one part schedule is split only when its days end before the whole time expression (`Mo-Fr 7 bis 22 Uhr`, `Mon-Fri 9-5 pm`, `Lun-Ven 9-18h`); an hour range with its unit is one expression. Daily, weekday and weekend words of English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese are days (`Daily`, `Werktags`, `Tous les jours`, `Fines de semana`). Single letters are days only in a slash group in week order (`M/W/F`, `T/Th`) or a two letter range (`M-F`). A part with no clean cut is kept whole: in the times when it holds a time, else in the days (`By appointment`, `24/7`). The code `row_part_unclear` is gone; only a value over its field's limit drops the row (`row_value_over_limit`). No image rebuild needed.
