- **`rowPartsUnpaired` no longer counts a card row that holds text slots of its own** — Joomla T4 batch 1 readiness · `feat/joomla-t4-batch-1-readiness` · 2026-11-07
  **Affects:** template preparers reading `rowPartsUnpaired` on a template kit (`template-kit/lib/row-pairs.mjs`).
