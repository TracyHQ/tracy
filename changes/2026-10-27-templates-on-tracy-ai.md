- **The public Templates pages are served at `https://tracy.ai/templates/` through the tracy.ai Cloudflare Worker** · Akemi (Claude) · #1790 · `feat/templates-on-tracy-ai` · 2026-10-27
  **Affects:** visitors, search engines, and whoever runs demo sites. On production (`TRACY_ORIGIN=https://cowork.tracy.ai`) the Templates public origin now defaults to `https://tracy.ai` with no `.env` change; an explicit `TEMPLATES_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` still wins, and dev and laptops keep their own origin. Canonical, JSON-LD, sitemap and llms.txt state tracy.ai addresses. A direct `GET`/`HEAD` of `cowork.tracy.ai/templates*` answers 301 to the same path and query on tracy.ai, except the Worker's own subrequest (`CF-Worker: tracy.ai`), which is served the page; the 301 is `no-store` and both answers send `Vary: CF-Worker`, so a cached 301 can never reach the Worker (a redirect loop). Old `/design-inspiration` addresses move to tracy.ai in one hop; `/design-inspiration/api/*` and the page's files are unchanged. New `/templates/sitemap.xml` and `/templates/llms.txt`; `robots.txt` names the new sitemap. The fleet-managed `frame-ancestors` policy now lists the zone apex (`https://tracy.ai`) so a template page on tracy.ai can frame its demo; EmDash demos get the full managed policy instead of the bare origin.
  **Deploy debt:** the `tracy-web` and `tracy-host` images (the fleet scripts ship in the `tracy-host` image) rebuild on merge to `develop` and reach production with the next `develop` to `main` release. **Running demo sites do not change their header on deploy:** each needs a re-provision (`/ops` demos) or a signed `/_host/frame-policy` call for its label on its host. Until then the page on tracy.ai shows the screenshot instead of the live demo (the `demo-frame` probe reports it cannot be framed). `tracy.ai/robots.txt` belongs to the landing site and its Worker, not to this repository.
