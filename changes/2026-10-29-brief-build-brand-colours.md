The template's brand colour is measured from the stylesheets the site serves (T4 theme variables and T3 compiled LESS alike) and repainted by a `<style data-tracy-brand-colours>` in the existing head block (T4 custom module, T3 `snippet_close_head`); no template file or param is written, so the admin's theme settings still name the template colour. Icons drawn in the template's brand colour take the logo's at the same luminance, and picture prompts name the brand colours. Gavick templates (no T3/T4 head-code setting) and WordPress/EmDash engines are not covered yet.
