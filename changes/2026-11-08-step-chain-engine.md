- **A build of an allow-listed template runs the whole step chain on the server as one build** — Akemi Nguyen · #483 · `feat/step-chain-engine` · 2026-11-08
  **Affects:** `POST /api/sites` takes `workflow: 'step-chain/1'` with `researchRevisionId` (no `brief`) for a template on `STEP_CHAIN_TEMPLATES` while `STEP_CHAIN_ENABLED=1`; anything else builds exactly as before. The brief-build run frame (`sites/brief-build.js`) now picks its engine by that workflow and the template, and an engine may bring its own clocks (`limits`). The chain's model steps are runs of the planner, writer, picture and brand bench queues, so those queues must be running in the server (they are started by `server.js`); a bench holds one run at a time, so concurrent chain builds wait for each other step by step. Per-step minutes and USD and the ending land in `site_brief.timings.chain`. `STEP_CHAIN_KEEP_FILES=1` keeps a run's step files on disk for inspection.
