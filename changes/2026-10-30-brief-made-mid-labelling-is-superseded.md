- **A brief made while its photos were still being labelled is superseded once the labels move on.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** website opportunities owners and brief builds. Before, a Refresh pressed while the background photo labelling ran made a brief whose readiness was judged on missing labels (often `block`), and since the research key leaves vision labels out, every later Refresh answered that same blocked brief and the Details page called it up to date, so no Refresh button showed. Now `POST /revisions` makes a new brief when the held one was made mid-labelling (`assetLabelling.status` `labelling` or `stalled`) and the labelling has since settled or labelled more photos; the old brief keeps its content and leaves the research key. The Details page no longer reads such a brief as up to date, so it offers Refresh. A build whose action holds such a brief waits for the labelling (the same "Looking at photos" wait) and then builds from the new brief. No API shape changes. Rebuild the web image before the next pilot.
