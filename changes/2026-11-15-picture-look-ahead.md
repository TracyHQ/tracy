- **The picture step looks three photos ahead on the any-photo rung, so a gallery tile that tries many photos no longer waits one look per pass** — David (WO pipeline) · #633 · `perf/wo-picture-look-ahead` · 2026-11-15
  **Affects:** Rebuild and deploy tracy-web. Chain builds and picture bench runs: when a frame on the any-photo rung has no look yet for its next photo, its next three photos (`LOOK_AHEAD` in `site-pictures/ladder-step.js`) are looked at together, before the pass that places the first of them. The photos, their order, their cuts and their verdicts do not change. A photo looked at ahead and never placed keeps a `looker.calls` record marked `ahead`, and its cost (about $0.0003) is in the run's spend. A frame whose pictures are darkened when placed is not looked at ahead.
  **Why / Measured:** Local stand, Central Padel (12/11/2026): ten article frames each tried about 40 owner photos, and the first picture round spent all 40 ladder passes there (1169 s, about 29 s a pass, looks 9.7 s at the median, p90 15.8 s). Case REG-PR-633-picture-look-ahead PASS; every picture-step test (43 files) 270 pass, 0 fail.
