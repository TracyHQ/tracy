- **The brief decides how many records, video tiles and social items a site shows; the template only gives the layout.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds. The site plan (`brief-decides-items.mjs`, `site-plan-honesty.mjs`) now states each record list's count from the brief (`honesty.lists`): Joomla writes it as the articles module's `count`, and the existing current-item head rule hides the page's own item, so a related list shows every real sibling; a list with no brief records is taken off. A video tile with no playable brief video is taken off with the template's own list item (the block whole when none is left), and its words and picture are not written. A kept person's own social items are taken off whole (icon and link), and the business's social row no longer fills a person's list (`honesty.personSocials`). A bio, role or profile of the business's author or owner profile that the brief does not hold is emptied and never handed to the copy writer. A grid column left empty is hidden and its row centres the columns kept (`empty-frames.js`). Joomla only for the writes; WordPress and EmDash do not read `honesty.lists` or the new item hides yet. Video tiles stored as per-record fields (not a list) still fall back to a still picture. Rebuild the web image before the next brief build.
