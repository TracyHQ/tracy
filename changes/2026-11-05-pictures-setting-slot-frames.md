- **Site pictures: a frame the kit writes through a setting slot (the T4 page background) goes in siteSettings, so its package is no longer refused** · Claude, for Akemi · #PR · `fix/pictures-setting-slot-frames` · 2026-11-05
  **Affects:** whoever runs the picture step (step 8) or the write step on a T4 kit with `site.body-background`. Since the kit made the background frame `writable: true` with `slotId`, step 8 listed it in `frames`; the export then reported "the kit has no image slot" for it and its twins, and the write step refused the whole picture package (`BAD_FILE`). Step 8 now chooses the list with one rule (`inputs-index.js` `listOf`): not writable, or tied to a slot of `site.settingSlots`, goes in `siteSettings`; its twins get no entry, the setting paints them. Picture packages made before this fix on such a kit must be made again.
