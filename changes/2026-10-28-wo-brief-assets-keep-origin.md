- **Every brief asset now carries `source`, `credit` and `uploaderRole`, and brief validation rejects an asset without them** — Akemi · #47 · `feat/brief-readiness-labels` · 2026-10-28
  **Affects:** `packages/wo/website-opportunities` (`domain/pictures/asset-origin.mjs`, `buildBrief`, `validateBrief`, `brief-schema.json`, `src/brief-research.js`). A new brief revision lists, per asset, where the picture was collected (`source`), the attribution its source requires shown (`credit`, authors with their link, empty only when the source names no one) and who uploaded it (`uploaderRole`: owner, customer, unknown). Google Places photos now keep their author attribution from research into the brief (research used to carry the credit only for TripAdvisor and Google Maps pictures). `validateBrief` refuses an asset missing any of the three; a schema 1 brief, or a call with `{ legacy: true }`, is still accepted, so revisions stored before this still load. Anything that builds a brief by hand must set the three fields on each asset.
