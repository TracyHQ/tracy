- **Quickstart contract tools keep whole-unit slots writable and measurable, and skip a template's style-guide page when judging labels.** — Akemi · #97 · `fix/quickstart-tools-whole-units-demo-only` · 2026-10-28
  **Affects:** Joomla contract capture (`capture-contract.py --whole-units`), the content coverage audit (matcher, label rules R7 and R8, Slot fit) and the verify viewer. Contracts captured or audited from now on can differ: a unit with an image beside its words keeps its pieces; a page block that is a piece of a whole-unit slot now matches it; pieces of one HTML field are no longer R7 siblings; a slot inside a composite block gets a measured fit; a slot shown only through a link no longer trips viewer rule 4. New contract field: `"demoOnly": true` on a content-map entity (a style-guide page) leaves its slots out of R1 to R8 and out of the viewer's suspicion rules (README of `@tracy/quickstart-tools`, ADR 0040). Released contracts are unchanged; JA Boxing 1.0.8 (#46) is the first to use it.
