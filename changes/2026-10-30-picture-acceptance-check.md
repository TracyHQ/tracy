- **Every picture a brief build places passes an acceptance check: drawings are inspected for faults, crops keep people whole, frames keep their own topic, and uploads are weighed to their frame.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds on every CMS. The photo labeller that reads a drawing back is now told what the frame shows and names its faults in the same call (`defects`: garbled lettering, a distorted face or body, a visible seam or patch, another trade, another topic; `picture-acceptance.mjs`). A refused drawing is drawn once more with its fault named in the prompt, then the frame tries the owner's photos, then a neutral fill; every refusal and its reason stays on the receipt (`drawRefused`). People and clear faces drawn right are accepted. A crop of a photo with people in it keeps the whole subject box, so a head turned away is never cut (`cuts-the-subject` refuses the photo). A general frame whose copy names one offering never takes a photo linked to another (`other-topic`). Photos are written at most twice the widest box the template shows them in, and a JPEG over its byte budget is re-encoded at a lower quality (`picture-weight.js`, Joomla writer path). WordPress and EmDash writers do not yet run the weight step. Rebuild the web image before the next brief build.
