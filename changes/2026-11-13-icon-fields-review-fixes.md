- **Icon fields and picture frames, review findings: the icon step reads the page writer's real output, sees child list rows, says each twin's theme, and works for copy not written in English.** -- AI quickstart work for #575 (#595) | PR pending | `feat/ai-quickstart-575` | 2026-11-13
  **Affects:** The write step (#597) and the step chain wiring. `selectIcons` now reads `writePages`'s `pages[].fields` (entries `from: writer` or `from: data` holding a value); a page of the plan holding an icon field with no fields in the copy throws `icon_copy_missing`. Icon fields and picture frames inside child lists (`lists`, `itemLists`, nested) are now seen: a row is its own item, ids `<page>.<block>[.<item>].<list>.<n>.<field>`, one row per `items.max` (a row the write step leaves out drops its decisions). `declaredMediaOf` and `mediaAccountOf` (`picture-declared.mjs`) hold every icon, video and image instance the placed blocks declare to a decision. An icon field may declare `theme: "light" | "dark"`: it then gets only that version; undeclared twins keep both and the result notes `icon_theme_undeclared`. Social icons are the fields declaring `iconOf: "network"`; links without scheme, country domains and the networks' own short hosts find their network, ambiguous shorteners and bare handles are hidden with a reason. On a site whose plan language is not English, one call of the build model gives English keywords per item (draft prompt `icon-keywords-prompt.md`, approval in #600; `icon_keywords_failed` on a bad reply). `picturesStep` needs `model` (the text call) for content icons and never falls back to `vision` (`icon_ranker_missing`); it returns `notes` with the icon notes (`icons_all_hidden` when every content icon of a site with at least four is hidden) and `droppedFrames`. Frames are read exactly as Contract A declares them; any other shape throws `icon_frame_invalid` or `picture_frame_invalid`. ADR 0040 carries an amendment line for AI quickstart builds. Nothing at runtime calls the step yet: no image rebuild needed.
