- **Site check: demo words and pictures, and the remaining page measures** · Claude, for Akemi · #360, #361 · `feat/site-check-measures` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** whoever runs `node scripts/write-step-bench.mjs check`. The check now has page groups beside read-back: `demo-words` (block, step 6), `demo-pictures` (block, step 8), `demo-links` (block, step 3), `dead-links` (block, step 3), `empty-frames` (block, step 3), `cms-errors` (block, step 9), `text-overflow` (block, step 6), `tall-blocks` (warn, step 6), `stretched-pictures` (warn, step 8) and `unowned-slots` (warn, step 3). They run after read-back, on every kept page at 1440 and 390, and only when `runSiteCheck` gets a `host`; without one the result says `pageGroups: false` and the command prints "page groups: not run". The host's existing `demo-qa` measures do the browser work; the site is sent to it in calls of 15 pages (its cap per call), each with a 180 s budget. A new `bench-cli.js` hook reads `makeHost(siteKey)` and `baselines.read(contract)` from the command's dependencies, and `scripts/write-step-bench.mjs` supplies them (the baseline folder is `WRITE_BENCH_BASELINE_DIR`, outside the repo). New `scripts/site-check-baseline.mjs --site <untouched site> --version <contract> --pages-file <paths.json>` measures the demo baseline once per quickstart version and keeps it; later runs reuse it. `src/site-check/demo-baseline.js` can also build a weaker stopgap baseline from the kit. Demo groups are unmeasured, never green, when no baseline exists. Dead links, text overflow, tall blocks and stretched pictures are new measures that read a `pageMeasures` list the host does not return yet (`src/site-check/page-measures.js` lists its shape); until it does, those groups report unmeasured and only dead pages (from the host's `visited`) are found.
  **Why / Measured:** ADR 0044 decisions 7 and 8. Unit tests only, on invented pages judged by the host's own `demo-qa` code with a fake page reader; no site was read. Not yet measured: the baseline of an untouched JA Boxing site, the false-positive count of the demo words on a real run, that an untouched site is red, and the host's time on a 50-page site (6 s a page would be about 5 minutes in 4 calls).
