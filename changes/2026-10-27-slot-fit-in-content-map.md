- **Slot fit in the content map: capture measures each text and picture slot's shape, and a picture's keep flag follows its measured kind** — Akemi (Claude) · #15 · `feat/slot-fit-in-content-map` · 2026-10-27
  **Affects:** whoever packages a Joomla quickstart contract: the content coverage audit takes `--fit` (quickstart-contract skill, K8) and writes `fit`, `slotFitVersion` and `fitGroups` into `content-map.json` before `content-coverage.json`. The gate `slot-roles` now also validates Slot fit where a map declares it and counts slots without it. `readSlotRoles` returns `fit` beside the roles; the picture planner and the agent demo take a declared kind, transparency and box before any name or file guess. Released contracts are unchanged (ADR 0039). Builds on #8 (merged into this branch).
  **Why / Measured:** JA Boxing 1.0.5 kept 198 of 257 picture slots (`customize: "keep"` on every picture `--full-coverage` declares), so a site built from a brief kept mostly demo photos. Measured on the 1.0.6 demo with `--fit`: 177 photos and 15 video stills become replaceable, 64 stay kept (48 icons, 8 avatars, 2 avatar strips, 2 signatures, 2 cut-outs, 2 logos); the hero boxer is a cut-out with a transparent ground; 953 of 1264 text slots get measured limits.
