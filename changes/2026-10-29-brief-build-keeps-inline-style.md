- **Brief build replaces the words of a heading, never its style: accent words and line breaks of the demo are kept** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** every Joomla (T4 and T3) build written through the Joomla engine whose template mixes styles inside one text slot; the copy writer's prompt gains a `style` field; the demo-leftover check gains kind C20.
  - A slot whose demo value carries inline markup (`span`, `strong`, `em`, `b`, `i`, `mark`, `u`, `small`, `sup`, `sub`, `br`, with their classes and attributes) gets a style pattern: a JA ACM field from its `((span))` markers, a whole-unit HTML slot (a path that ends at an element) from the release's own rows (`presentation-lock.json`). `website-opportunities/domain/inline-style.mjs`, `sites/brief-engines/inline-styles.js`.
  - The writer is shown the pattern (`"style": "THE[[br]][[WORKOUT]]"`) and marks the key words of its own sentence (`WHAT WE [[COACH]]`). Words with no fitting marks are styled by position (the last word when the demo styled its last word, the break where the demo broke). Only the demo's own tags are written; the writer's text is escaped and any markup in it is dropped.
  - A JA ACM field takes its markers through the content contract. A whole-unit HTML slot is written as plain words by the contract (it takes words only and its whole-unit write removes inline markup), then the demo's tags are put back at the same path by the records step after it, merged with the other module and article writes and checked by the bound-nodes guard. `sites/brief-engines/joomla-inline-styles.js`.
  - After every write the stored values are read back; a styled slot that lost its style is named in the build notes and counted (`inlineStyleLost`, `inlineStylesKept`). The leftover check reports the same as C20 when it is given the stored values (`styleReadback`).
  **Why / Measured:** on a local brief build of a JA T4 template the section title "THE<br><span>WORKOUT</span>" became the plain "WHAT WE COACH": the line break and the accent word were gone. 197 slots of 21 of the 36 Joomla content maps in the repo carry such styling (132 JA ACM marker fields, 65 whole-unit HTML slots).
