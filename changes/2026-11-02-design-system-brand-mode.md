- **Brand mode for the Joomla Design system slot, and three BRAND readiness rules shown on /ops** — Akemi (with Claude) · `feat/design-system-brand-mode` · 2026-11-02
  **Affects:** `@tracy/ds-slot`: `brandProfile` builds a profile from a customer's `{ bg, fg, accent, fonts }`, and `joomlaSlot(…, { brand: true })` hands the accent role of every kept band to the brand accent. On every template, in brand mode only, accent words reach 4.5:1 and accent fills (buttons, their borders) 3:1, on the page and in each kept band, by a lightness shift that keeps the brand hue (never the body text colour); each shift is recorded in `adjusted`. `ds-slot.mjs joomla` and `flow.mjs` take `brand:<bg>-<fg>-<accent>` ids; `scripts/brand-measure.mjs` measures a stand and writes `brand-measurement.json`. Without `brand` the module is byte for byte unchanged. The readiness check gains BRAND-1, BRAND-2 (static) and BRAND-3 (scored from `brand-measurement.json`, live-only with its command until then); /ops lists them under their own heading on the Design system step, and they never change the grade or ds-ready. ADR 0031 revision 02/11/2026 and the tdsr skill describe them.
