- **Size the build's picture clock by the photos left to label, label them four batches at a time, and read only the photos a slot takes.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** quickstart brief builds. The picture phase's clock is no longer a fixed 3 minutes: the engine counts the photos still without a label and asks for 15 s plus one batch time (60 s until measured) per round of 4 batches of 8, held between 1 and 8 minutes, so a build with nothing to label moves on quickly. Preparation and apply now pick the photo shortlist by the same frame needs, so a photo labelled in preparation is never counted as missed in apply. Each batch's duration is kept in the build's `photoLabels` receipt (`batchMs`). Apply reads the bytes of only the photos its plan places, not of every stored photo first; a chosen photo whose bytes are gone is set aside and the plan made again. Rebuild the web image before the next brief build.
