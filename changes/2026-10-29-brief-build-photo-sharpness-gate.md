- **Brief build sharpness gate: an owner photo keeps a picture slot only when it stays sharp there, cut and shown at 1x and 2x** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** every brief build that places owner photos (Joomla fill plan path), and every drawing of a brief build.
  - Each owner photo the fill plan places is cut for its slot and rendered at the slot's shown width at 1x and 2x (`lib/photo-sharpness.js`): enlargement past the slot class's limit, a low fine-detail share, JPEG blocks (read on the cut and on its source) or a blurred, stretched edge band fail it. Classes by shown width: small (up to 400 px) tolerates the most enlargement, large (over 600 px) the least; a full-width hero background (under the page's words) takes the small bars.
  - A soft or mildly blocky photo gets a light correction (3 px median, mild unsharp mask; never an AI upscale) and is measured again; still failing, it is refused for that slot (`unsharp` in `planFill`), the next owner photo of the ranking is met, and with none left the slot is drawn as a scene (never extended from the refused photo, `planDrawings`).
  - A drawing whose edge comes back as a blurred, stretched copy of the scene is refused (`blurred-region`) and drawn once more; a cached drawing with such a band is not reused.
  - Every slot's receipt carries `sharpness`: score, threshold, the step taken (kept, corrected, no sharp photo) and the photos refused with their scores.
  **Why / Measured:** on a local brief build the class card of one offering showed a 567 px social photo enlarged into a 704 px card (2.5x on a retina screen), visibly soft; the pipeline checked only pixel width. Another class card showed a drawing whose left fifth was a blurred, stretched copy of the scene.
