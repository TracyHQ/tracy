- **Pictures: a picture is reused only in the same shape on another page, the size tolerance holds per side, and initials fold stroke letters and drop roles.** -- AI quickstart work for #575 (#595) | PR pending | `feat/ai-quickstart-575` | 2026-11-13
  **Affects:** The pictures step and the write step (#597). New `domain/picture-reuse.mjs` (`shapeClassOf`, `mayReuse`, `reuseProblemsOf`): a drawing or an own photo of an item shows again only in a frame of the same shape class (portrait, landscape, square by the declared size) on another page; a frame without a declared size reuses nothing; an unused own photo of the item comes first; the second frame of an item on one page takes another own photo, else its own drawing (a person: their initials). `assignPictures` throws `picture_policy_broken` with `reuse_shape_unfit` or `reuse_same_page` if a later change breaks it. More drawings result: the approved count of a plan counts them (`picturesOf`). `unfitReason`: the two pixel tolerance applies to the width and the height each on its own (1440x510 fits 1440x512; 100x1000 does not fit 102x1020). `initialsOf`: O with stroke, L with stroke, AE, OE, D with stroke and kin fold to their base letter; text in parentheses or after a comma is left out; a Thai or Lao leading vowel is skipped; the initials of a person item read its `person.name` field before its display label.
