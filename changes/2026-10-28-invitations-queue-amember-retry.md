- **An Invitations send queue waits out aMember outages for 30 minutes before pausing, and tells the ops topic whenever the cron pauses it** — Sarah (Claude) · #30 · `fix/invitations-queue-amember-retry` · 2026-10-28
  **Affects:** tracy-web (`invitations/real-queue.js`). When aMember does not answer (`REAL_MEMBERSHIP_UNKNOWN`) the cron pass waits and asks again on a later pass instead of pausing the queue; nothing is sent or skipped while it waits. It pauses only after `AMEMBER_RETRY_MS` (30 minutes) of failures with no clear answer in between; a send that goes out or a customer refused by name clears the failing run, and so do pause, start and resume. Migration **181** adds `invitations_real_queue.amember_failing_since`. Every pause the cron makes (for any reason) records `invitations.queue_paused {campaign_id, reason}`, which is in `NOTIFY` and reaches the ops Telegram topic. Gmail not answering, the budget reached and sending turned off still pause at once.
  **Why / Measured:** on the nights of 26-27/10 and 27-28/10 both live queues (Snapshot 07/10 and JomSocial win-back) paused between 00:01 and 00:06 Vietnam time because aMember did not answer, then lay still for 9 to 33 hours until someone resumed them. Why aMember fails at that hour is not verified.
