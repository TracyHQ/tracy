  **Affects:** The plan rules and the page writer for a list with a days field and a times field. A one part schedule is split only when the whole text is one day group, one separator and one time expression (a clock time or a range of two, with an optional leading word such as `from`, `ab`, `until`, `dalle`, kept with the times: `Mon-Fri until 5pm`, `Sat 9 - noon`, `Mo-Fr ab 18 Uhr`). Anything else is kept whole and unchanged: in the times when it holds a digit (`Mon-Fri 9-5, Sat 10-2`, `Kids 6 to 12`, `24/7`), else in the days (`By appointment`). Only a value over its field's limit drops the row. No image rebuild needed.
