- **The plan no longer tells a customer "No logo was found" when research found their logo but the brand step could not use it.** -- AI quickstart work for #575 (#616) | PR pending | `feat/ai-quickstart-575` | 2026-11-14
  **Affects:** The plan card's look section. When the brand step runs out of review rounds it writes its safe version (`logo.material: null`, a lettermark), and the look record read the logo only from that material, so a business whose logo research had found was told "No logo was found" and was given the template's accent as "you have no logo". `brandLookOf` and `accentOfBrandFile` now also read the logo research found (`logoResearch.chosen.mediaId`, else `brand.logo`, new export `researchLogoIdOf`). "No logo was found" and the template accent source are for a business whose research found no logo at all; a found logo the step did not use says "Your logo could not be used as it is" and its accent follows the logo rules (black and white, adjusted, or the logo's own colour). A found logo whose colours were never measured now stops the plan with the existing `brand_logo_colours_unmeasured` error instead of showing the template accent. Nothing at runtime outside the plan step reads this: no image rebuild needed.
