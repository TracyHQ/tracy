- **The write step hides a template's hard coded article blocks, such as a share bar, on the pages it creates only (card AT)** — Write step · #415 · `fix/pictures-never-leave-frame-empty` · 2026-11-06
  **Affects:** template kits gain `site.createdPageHide` (`template-kit/cli.mjs created-page-hide --kit <file> --selector '<css>' --why '<where>'`, or `--none`); the JA Boxing 1.0.11 kit names `.share-social`. After the contract call the write step writes, between `tracy-created-hide` markers in the kit's head injection block, one rule per created page's menu item scoped by T4's body classes (`write-step/created-page-hide-stage.js`); the look guard allows only such rules (`created-page-hide` exception). `createPages` now also answers `opened` (the menu item opening each created page). A kit that names nothing behaves as before. ADR 0044 decision 29 covers this fourth use.
  **Why / Measured:** card AT: on a bench site with the JA Boxing 1.0.11 kit the created Privacy page (printed through `ja_boxing:default`, card AN) showed Facebook, X and Reddit share buttons; `html/com_content/article/default.php` prints `div.share-social` hard coded, and no article layout of the template lacks both it and the action bar. Step 11 `created-controls` blocked at 1440 and 390.
