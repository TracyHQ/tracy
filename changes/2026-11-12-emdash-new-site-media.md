- **Apply keeps the pictures the importer placed on an EmDash site: a seed picture path at a media-map spot no longer replaces the imported picture.** -- EmDash new-site pictures | #572 | `fix/emdash-new-site-media` | 2026-11-12
  **Affects:** Every EmDash template (one shared `tools/personalize.mjs`); Restaurant and Diner are the two whose seeds hold such paths. Reaches customer sites only once the tracy-web image is rebuilt and deployed. Sites already personalized keep their broken pictures until Apply runs again or the pictures are repaired.
  **Why / Measured:** New EmDash sites made through Add site from JA Restaurant 1.0.2 showed broken pictures at `/images/joomlart/...` (8 on Home, 17 on Menu) after Apply, while the never-personalized demo site served them from `/_emdash/api/media/file/`. Cause: the Restaurant and Diner seeds keep the source path at each spot `seed/media-map.json` fills; Apply rewrites lists from the seed's rows, and `keepSiteRowKeys` lets a key the written row names win, so the path replaced the imported picture. Fix: `keepImportedPictures` puts the site's picture back for any seed path at a media-map spot before the write. Regression case REG-PR-572-emdash-apply-keeps-imported-pictures.
