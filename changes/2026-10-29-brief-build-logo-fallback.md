- **A brief build's logo spots show the owner's logo or the business name, never the template's logo** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** Joomla brief builds (T3 and T4) whose site plan says `brief-logo` or `name-as-text`. A logo the site will not take (an upload or a style refused, a T4 profile write refused) leaves that spot on the business name as text; a logo narrower than the footer slot's `fit.minWidth` is set at its own size on a transparent canvas that wide, or shows the name when it would show under 16 pixels high; and every module whose HTML still prints one of the template's logo files (`logo.demoFiles`) shows the name in its place. Builds without a brief keep the template's logo when a logo is refused, as before.
  **Why / Measured:** on a local brief build on a Joomla T4 template the header and footer kept the demo logo files; the footer logo slot asks for a picture at least 1.5 times its box, wider than many owner logos.
