- **The site writer can write its first draft in parallel parts, and a chain build's copy step writes it in three** — David (WO pipeline) · #633 · `perf/wo-picture-look-ahead` · 2026-11-15
  **Affects:** Rebuild and deploy tracy-web. Chain builds' copy step now passes `--parallel-pages 3`; a writer bench run started by hand changes nothing unless started with `--parallel-pages <n>` (2 to 8, frozen as `harness.parallelPages`; 1 or absent is today's single first-draft call, byte for byte, and freezes nothing). With it, the writing order is cut into n runs of consecutive pages of about the same size (`site-writer/writer-parts.js`), each written in its own model conversation at the same time: the same fixed part, its own pages, the pages the other parts write, the site voice, the business's own phrases shared out one part each, and the phrases two parts' plan notes share kept by the first part and reworded by the other. The answers are merged in part order and checked once; the fix rounds continue the last part's conversation. Each part saves its calls in its own turn range (`lib/bench-run-parts.js`, n = part × 1000), so `turns.jsonl` and `status` show turn numbers 0, 1000, 2000… for such a run; a restart resumes each part from its saved turns, a cancel stops them all, the run's cost is the sum of every call, and the USD fuse applies per part conversation, as per writing turn. It cannot be combined with `--pages-per-turn`. The chain build's copy step does not pass it: turn it on there only once a stand run on the same plan measures it equal or better than one call.
  **Why / Measured:** Local stand, Central Padel run12 (15/11/2026): the first draft was one model turn of 451 s (105.7k output tokens, 88.7k of them reasoning, for about 17k tokens of copy), then fix rounds of 119 s, 32 s and 55 s. Same plan (run13's), one run each: three parts took 509 s against 594 s for today's single call, with 27 first-draft failures against 79 (W06 repeats 18 against 66) and 3 failures standing at the end against 11; the copy step spent 0.19 USD against 0.09. Tests `site-writer-bench-parallel.test.mjs` 14 pass; every `site-writer-*` and `bench*` test file 126 pass, every `*bench*` test file 304 pass, 0 fail. Case REG-PR-633-copy-parallel-pages.
