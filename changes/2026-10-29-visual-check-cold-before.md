- **The visual check's early "before" survives a cold site container** — David (Claude) · #<pr> · `fix/visual-check-2c` · 2026-10-29

**Affects:** every customer edit on a WordPress, Joomla or EmDash site whose container started recently (the first turn after a deploy, a restart or an idle stop). Needs a tracy-dsh image rebuild to reach the fleet.

- **Cause, reproduced on the local stand (29/10/2026):** the first turn on a just-started WordPress and Joomla site closed `no-before-capture-LOOK_FAILED` and `no-before-capture-timeout`. Debug logs of the early picture showed `TRACY_BROWSER ERR timed out` after 21.6 s on both sites: chromium alone started in 7.5 s cold against 3.7 s warm, and the page, the 5 s settle and the agent's own first page read on the same container pushed the picture past the view deadline of 16 s. The same picture on the warm site took 4 to 5 s, through the docker proxy as dsh runs it.
- **Fix:** the early picture is drawn while the agent is still reading and nobody waits on it, so it now has its own deadline (`VIEW_COLD_DEADLINE_MS`, 40 s), on the site's browser and on dsh's host fallback; the in-container `timeout` backstop and the docker client's timeout keep their margin over it. Every other picture keeps 16 s, and the write hold still waits at most 10 s.
- **After the fix, same stand, both sites restarted cold:** both early pictures were drawn (10 to 12 s), the write was held with `missing: null`, and both turns were judged (`shown` on Joomla; `not_shown` on WordPress, a real verdict on a heading the agent changed elsewhere).
- **Beta report item 2c, the 10 bare `no-before` turns on prod1:** the early picture, the 10 s hold and the reasons on every missing "before" (`56e3ee678`, 23/10) reached production only with release #1760 on 26/10/2026 10:05 UTC. Turns between 23/10 16:00 (UTC+7) and then ran the old 5 s hold, measured at 42/42 `no-before` on 19–23/10, which cut every reason to a bare `no-before`.
