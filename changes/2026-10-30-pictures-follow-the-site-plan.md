- **Pictures and icons follow the accepted site plan: each picture slot takes the plan's brief photo, a drawing from the plan's description, or is hidden; each icon takes the plan's meaning or one neutral icon for its row.** — build maintainers · #239 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief build maintainers (spec #231). With an accepted plan, the Joomla picture stage (`site-plan-pictures.js`, `real-pictures.js`) pins every slot to its planned photo through the existing acceptance and crop rules (`planFill` takes `only`); a refused planned photo, a hide, or a slot with no usable decision is cleared and taken off, recorded. A picture is drawn only where the plan draws, told the plan's description (`planDrawings` takes `described`; `subject.describe` in the drawing prompt). A photo the plan does not use is never placed, extended or drawn from. What was applied per picture and icon slot is kept on the build record (`sitePlanPictures`). Without a plan nothing changes. WordPress and EmDash are not covered yet.
