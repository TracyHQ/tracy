- **An EmDash release's slot roles are read from its theme's field map by the Road to live, and JA Diner and JA Restaurant declare theirs** — hano-xbt · #TBD · `chore/emdash-road-open` · 2026-11-09
  **Affects:** Road to live step Content coverage for every EmDash release; people packaging an EmDash template; the `slot-roles` gate. `rolesOfRead` (`content-coverage/roles.js`) ignored the `field-map` source of the shared reader, so every EmDash release read "no roles" (`coverage-roles` todo, or `coverage-live-roles` warn on a published one) although the reader said "ready". It now returns ready, missing (fields without a role) or invalid. The roles reader (`emdash-field-map-roles.js`) also reads the shape `compile-seed.mjs` writes (`fields` keyed `<collection>.<field>`, types and repeater sub-fields taken from `seed.json`), and `isCopylessField` exempts layout settings and links (`view`, `path`, `section_style`, `*_color`, `*_icon`, `*_link`, `position` on its own). `seed/field-map.json` of `tracy-emdash-theme-diner` and `tracy-emdash-theme-restaurant` now carries `roleVocabularyVersion`, `settings` and a role on every copy field; `artifact.json` of both is regenerated. JA SaaS (same shape) still declares none. The Template kit step is unchanged: nothing builds a kit for an EmDash release yet.
