- **A call-to-action band is one unit: its message is written for its button's action** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** Joomla T3/T4 brief builds and quickstart packing. Content maps declare call-to-action bands as `ctaBands` (message slots with a heading or line part, declared buttons, ground picture); `declare-actions.mjs` proposes them and the packing check refuses an undeclared band or one with no message slot role. JA Boxing 1.0.8 declares its home band. After every copy pin and cleanup, the build asks the text model for the band's heading and line from the brief and the action its button carries (numbers, prices, dashes, demo words and offers the brief does not state are refused and asked again), keeps them through the record cleanup, empties a line that cannot be written, and takes a band whose heading cannot be written off whole. The build check reports a kept band that shows a button with no message. WordPress and EmDash maps declare no bands yet. Requires a web image rebuild.
