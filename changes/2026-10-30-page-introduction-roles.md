- **Page introductions follow their declared page roles** — Akemi (Codex) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds with declared mastheads, hero sublines or native page leads. Team and offering pages no longer inherit a location introduction from an About or Contact ancestor. English introductions are checked before contract writes, and the final rendered-page check rejects mismatched copy across CMS frameworks. True location facts remain on About, Contact and place pages. A masthead's default line that several pages of different roles share keeps the business's own copy instead of one page's role or place line, a place line follows the contact page's own role line, and the final check fails a page without its own planned intro only on a place or contact line.
