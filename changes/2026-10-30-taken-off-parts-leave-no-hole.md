- **A part a brief build takes off leaves no hole** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** Joomla brief builds (T3 and T4 alike, any module or article). When the records door hides a picture, link or list item for lack of brief content (the `hidden` attribute), the empty-frame pass no longer counts it as content: the wrapper it leaves (its margins and the blank line of its emptied words) is hidden too, so the neighbours close up with their normal spacing. Such a wrapper is named by its hidden picture from the nearest id, so the rule still matches when a template script moves the block into another column after load.
