- **WordPress capture: theme text options and template part entities; coverage accepts a priced menu item name** · Akemi0x · TracyHQ/tracy#459 (spec #452) · `feat/wp-459-diner-wp7-release` · 2026-11-08
  **Affects:** whoever captures a WordPress contract. `capture-contract.mjs --theme-text-options` declares `manifest.themeTexts` (options with the theme's own prefix) and makes an option entity with one text slot of each; `--template-parts` makes a `templatePart` entity (identity `{slug}`) with the slots of the part's named blocks, and `capture-inventory.mjs` locks it by its skeleton. Both off by default, so released profiles re-capture byte for byte. Claude Cowork 0.18.40 or later binds them. The label rule R2 accepts a price inside an inline markup slot whose role is `offering.name` or `package.name`; a plain text name with a price still fails.
  **Why / Measured:** JA Diner WP7 1.0.2 had five fact holes with no door (the footer contact line, the Contact phone and address, the Reservation phone). The theme now prints them from five options through identity tokens, byte for byte as before, and the contract carries them as slots.
