- **The write step now hides for real: dropped blocks and pages leave the site, hidden slots are emptied, and what the plugin cannot take is reported as failed** · Claude, for Akemi · #362 · `feat/write-step-hide` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** the write step bench (`bench-cli`, /ops), the site check and the content seeder's Joomla apply. A new stage (`write-step/hide-stage.js`, `hide-plan.js`, `hide-writes.js`, `hide-lines.js`) runs before the brand. It reads `info.hide_ops` and, on a plugin that has it, sends `blocks.unpublish` for blocks the plan drops whole, `setSource` for fixed sentences the plan names by the kit's id, and hands the other hidden slots to the one contract call as `empty_slots`. Slots the plugin refuses are dropped and retried like changes, each failed in its words. A slot addressed by a text node or attribute, a whole column and a slot that does not exist are failed naming #369. An older plugin takes nothing and every hide line is failed. Dropped pages lose their menu item first; main menu items stay with the page settings stage, so nothing is unpublished twice, and an item a kept page or kept link depends on stays published and is reported failed. New report lines: `hidden`, plus `part: block`, `page-drop` and `fixed-text`. Read-back reads a slot gone with its block or menu item on that row's `published`. `applyJoomlaContract` takes `mapping.emptySlots`; a mapping without it replays under the same request id.
  **Why / Measured:** hide lines were reported skipped and demo words stayed on the page. Run offline on a frozen plan and kit with a fake door applying the plugin's rules: 242 hide lines became 173 hidden and 69 failed, 4 blocks unpublished, 19 menu items unpublished once each, 121 slots emptied in one contract call. Whether emptying a whole row leaves a hole, and everything on a real site, is not measured here.
