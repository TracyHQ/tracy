- **`phoneNotCallable` no longer counts a phone slot that holds a `tel:` address** — Joomla T4 batch 1 readiness · #625 · `feat/joomla-t4-batch-1-readiness` · 2026-11-07
  **Affects:** template preparers reading `phoneNotCallable` on a template kit (`template-kit/lib/reach-checks.mjs`). A content map may give the link's `href` its own `contact.phone` slot; that slot is the address that makes the number callable, not a number printed outside a link. A phone slot whose words are a number in no link is still counted.
  **Why / Measured:** JA Vega j6 1.0.10 module 126: `<a href="tel:000">951-925-0780</a>` read as two `contact.phone` slots, and the `href` slot (`tel:000`) was counted. New test in `test/template-kit-reach-checks.test.mjs` fails before the fix and passes after (6 pass, 0 fail).
