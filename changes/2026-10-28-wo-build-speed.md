- **Website Opportunities research and site builds wait less: work that does not depend on other work runs at the same time** — David (Claude) · #29 · `perf/wo-build-speed` · 2026-10-28
  **Affects:** people waiting on a demo or on Build from my business, and operators of `tracy-host`. A build's check sends its vision calls while the browser goes on capturing (three in flight, applied in tile order, so the verdict is the serial one); a host with the memory for it runs up to three demo QA runs and vision captures at once and reads a check's pages three at a time (`apps/tracy-host/src/browser-room.js`: one per 8 GB of the container's memory, three tabs from 16 GB; `TRACY_BROWSER_RUNS` and `TRACY_DEMO_QA_TABS` override; a Mac stand keeps one of each); a build's page words are written four pages at a time instead of two. One check's sweep rounds (up to six before it is counted) no longer run the vision look, whose verdict an uncounted round threw away, and read only the pages that changed; a look names the pages it saw whole (`seen`), so a site of ~50 pages is covered across checks instead of restarting every look at its home page and never passing `looks-finished`. A page lead that no record field and no contract slot holds (JA Fresco prints its contact lead from a template language string) is no longer required in the build's plan: the gate asked for a row no target could satisfy, and a Joomla build ended with no plan; such a line is left to the sweep. Research downloads a place's pictures five at a time (a video alone) and keeps them in list order, and reads the PageSpeed audit, the menu and the business together. Each check logs `[demo-check] … inventory, page reads, look` seconds and each research run `[opportunities] place job …` per stage, so the next measure on a server can say where the minutes go.
  **Deploy debt:** `tracy-web` and `tracy-host` images, rebuilt on merge to `develop`; production with the next `develop` to `main` release. Vision, PageSpeed and business-reading charges can now be reserved together, so a research run right at its cost cap may pause one step earlier than before.
