  **Affects:** people waiting on a demo or on Build from my business, and operators of `tracy-host`. A build's check sends its vision calls while the browser goes on capturing (three in flight, applied in tile order, so the verdict is the serial one); a host with the memory for it runs up to three demo QA runs and vision captures at once and reads a check's pages three at a time (`apps/tracy-host/src/browser-room.js`: one per 8 GB of the container's memory, three tabs from 16 GB; `TRACY_BROWSER_RUNS` and `TRACY_DEMO_QA_TABS` override; a Mac stand keeps one of each); a build's page words are written four pages at a time instead of two. Each check logs `[demo-check] … inventory, page reads, look` seconds and each research run `[opportunities] place job …` per stage, so the next measure on a server can say where the minutes go.
