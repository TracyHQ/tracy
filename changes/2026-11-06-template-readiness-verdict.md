  **Affects:** Road to live and the publish door (`apps/tracy-web/src/lib/road-to-live.js`): the release, install file, contract, Content coverage, Template kit, Customize your site and Design system steps are now built by `src/lib/template-readiness/verdict.js` (`verdictSteps`), with the same states, codes and params as before (the Road to live tests are unchanged and pass).
