- **JA Diner and JA Restaurant (EmDash) get a generated content map and a measured content coverage, and Road to live no longer answers "not needed" for an EmDash release** — hano-xbt · `feat/emdash-template-readiness` · 2026-11-09
  **Affects:** Road to live step Content coverage for every EmDash release; anyone changing an EmDash theme's seed, field map or content API map; whoever readies an EmDash template. Each theme's `contracts/<version>/` now holds `content-map.json` (GENERATED by `packages/cms/tracy-quickstart-tools/content-coverage/emdash-content-map.mjs --theme <folder> --write` from `seed/seed.json`, `seed/field-map.json` and `seed/content-api-map.json`; never edit it by hand) and `content-coverage.json` (written by `emdash-audit.mjs`, which binds what a running site shows to the map through the provenance stamps of a `X-Tracy-Preview: pick` request instead of matching words across the contract). New gate `emdash-content-map` fails when the committed map is not what the theme's files generate (the Slot fit the audit measured into it is ignored) or when the coverage file names another map: change a field role or an entry, run the generator, then measure again. `policy.json` now measures `emdash` for holes, page problems, Slot fit and label rules; `coverage-not-needed` is left to a platform with no content map at all, and a platform that binds roles but has no audit answers the new `coverage-roles-only`. A release's artifacts (content map, coverage, kit, demo baseline) are found beside an EmDash theme's contract data through `contractOfRelease` (`slot-roles-of-release.js`); `contractFor` and the profile index are unchanged and no `manifest.json` is added. The coverage reader accepts a contract without a presentation lock (an EmDash theme has none).
  **Why / Measured:** the two EmDash releases were checked for nothing: `emdash` was in `rolesPlatforms` only, so the step was `done coverage-not-needed` whatever the pages showed. The audit binds text through the stamps, pictures only through the site's own REST media index (`--token-file`, required: a stamp names the entry, not the file), and measures every visible link as an item (bound to a link slot, menu, declared page or taxonomy term, else a hole). It crawls the pages the map declares and every same-site page they link to (28 + 5 and 37 + 6 pages, desktop and phone); one outside the map is audited like any other and counted in `pages.notInMap`. The generator now makes a field a slot when the field map gives it a role even if its name looks like a layout setting; `seed/field-map.json` of `tracy-emdash-theme-restaurant` gives `menu.items.dish_extra_class` (the "Hot" and "Sale" badge printed beside a dish) the role `offering.variant_meta` (artifact.json and the inventory regenerated). Measured: JA Diner 0.1.0 has nothing left (`coverage-ready`); JA Restaurant 1.0.1 has no unexplained piece either but holds (`coverage-holes`, incomplete) on one dead link: `/pages`, the category link of the post `/pages/chefs`. Count and page-number wording the templates print ("# articles", "Page # of #") is accepted in the coverage files as interface wording. The label rules are computed but not enforced: both themes are on `place-content-roles/2` and the rules apply to `/3`.
