- **The template kit reads every link and icon of a block, judges short rows by the content around them, and JA Boxing 1.0.10 ships an approved kit** — Claude, for Akemi · `feat/template-kit` · 2026-11-02
  **Affects:** anyone running `template-kit/cli.mjs build` or reading `template-kit.json`, and the Template kit step of `quickstart/ja-boxing/j6` on the Road to live. The build shoots only the images that go in the pack and re-reads everything else in the DOM; a reused capture shoots only what a new content map adds. In `template-kit.json`: list rows come from the map's slot groups (`rows[]`, `maxFrom: "slot-rows"`); every shown link is listed by `position` (repeated links included) with the icon it draws, and a row icon names the content map slot that holds it as its `field`; links carry an `action` (`contact`, `external-profile`, and the others); fixed text carries a measured `canHide`, CMS language strings name every constant with their value, and copy that is really another item's content moves to `demoOwned`; listing and author pages take the map's items lists and card fields (`checks.itemsListsWithoutCardFields`, which must be 0). Planner rules, each with a check that must be 0 or the Road to live step reads `kit-incomplete`: one character limit per slot kind of a list, measured by filling the frame (`checks.listLimitsDiffering`, `text-fit.json`); template showcase pages (demo-only content, a search results page, picture-only pages in their section) leave the kit and are named in `excludedPages` (`checks.showcasePagesNotExcluded`); page groups follow the real structure, a listing with its own items and the detail pages its list links to, represented by the listing page (`checks.mixedPageGroups`, `checks.detailPagesOutsideListing`, `checks.representativesNotListing`); a slot belongs to the one block that renders it (`checks.slotsInTwoBlocks`); every fixed text has a stable `id` in the planner's `{id, text, canHide}` shape (`checks.fixedTextIdsInvalid`). Every text a block shows has a slot (`checks.orphanTexts`): a block's own titles, intros and lone button labels, the CMS strings and form labels it prints, and the menu item title a page prints as its heading are listed in `blocks[].slots[]`, a menu link names its label slot (`links[].labelId`), and another item's words that only coincide (`Next` in a typography article) are fixed text. A large partly clear picture is a `cutout` frame even when nothing lies under it. A contact block showing a CMS contact says what it accepts (`site.contactBlocks[].accepts`): its one-value fields, whether the page prints each, and where a messaging app or social profile goes, or `none` when named links are off. The read pass records every shown text, a paragraph longer than 300 characters included, and every shown paragraph whole; a slot shown in several paragraphs must have each one read (`checks.paragraphsMissing`); `--reuse-capture` reads the DOM again with the current reader and keeps the shots. Short CMS words printed beside a longer string (`Prev`/`Next` of the article navigation) are now found and measured, and a value shared by several language files is named after the template's or the CMS's own constant before an add-on's. A short last row pressed to the left is a template gap only when the content around it is centred; a short row in a left-aligned column, in line with the words beside it, is right. A coverage gate completes the kit from the contract and the build's page readings and must read 0 (`checks.slotCoverage`, parts and every failure in `coverage`; /ops names the failing parts and slots): every content map text slot a kit page shows is listed with one `maxCharacters` and its `maxCharactersFrom`, the same in every place and never below the demo words; the limit comes from filling the slot's real container (a tab, chip, menu label or button included) until it wraps past the lines its group shows or pushes another text, while a content map fit that is only the demo words' box is no limit and is never lent to a sibling; every link's label is a slot of the block holding it (`ownedBy` when another block owns it) and says what else changes with it (`alsoChanges`); the main menu group has a whole-row limit read with every top-level label filled together at each width down to where the row folds (`rowLimit`, `row-fit.json`); every text slot, the row template of a list with no sample row included, gives its `paragraphs`, `bullets` and the first words of each; and each contact field names its slot or says `writable: false` with the reason (the mobile, fax and website lines have no contract column). The JA Boxing 1.0.10 contract is re-sealed with author article lists and the footer icon slots, and its approved `template-kit.json` sits beside it.
  **Why / Measured:** on a local stand restored from the 1.0.10 installer the kit reads 50 pages in 12 groups (four template showcase pages left out by structure), 76 blocks and 110 images, 0 blocks without a shot, all eleven self-checks at 0 (items lists without card fields, list limits differing, showcase pages kept, mixed page groups, detail pages outside their listing, representatives that are not the listing, slots in two blocks, orphan texts (595 shown texts without a declared slot before this change), paragraphs missing (14 slots before this change: a quote and 13 article bodies with a paragraph over 300 characters the read pass dropped), fixed text ids, and the coverage gate over 639 shown text slots, its eight parts at 0; the main menu row holds 51 characters, read from 1440 down to 992 where it folds, 548 block slots plus 25 link labels listed by reference, 0 open template gaps (31 accepted placeholder links), 170 fixed texts with ids (the article navigation's `Prev`/`Next` now among them, named by Joomla's own constants), and 2 measurement errors left (two lists with no row set of their size at desktop width). A person approved its image set; the block slot, paragraph, frame kind and contact changes leave that set unchanged, so the approval still holds. The template-kit and content-coverage test files pass one file at a time.
