- **The template kit reads every link and icon of a block, judges short rows by the content around them, and JA Boxing 1.0.10 ships an approved kit** — Claude, for Akemi · `feat/template-kit` · 2026-11-02
  **Affects:** anyone running `template-kit/cli.mjs build` or reading `template-kit.json`, and the Template kit step of `quickstart/ja-boxing/j6` on the Road to live. The build shoots only the images that go in the pack and re-reads everything else in the DOM; a reused capture shoots only what a new content map adds. In `template-kit.json`: list rows come from the map's slot groups (`rows[]`, `maxFrom: "slot-rows"`); every shown link is listed by `position` (repeated links included) with the icon it draws, and a row icon names the content map slot that holds it as its `field`; links carry an `action` (`contact`, `external-profile`, and the others); fixed text carries a measured `canHide`, CMS language strings name every constant with their value, and copy that is really another item's content moves to `demoOwned`; listing and author pages take the map's items lists and card fields (`checks.itemsListsWithoutCardFields`, which must be 0). Planner rules, each with a check that must be 0 or the Road to live step reads `kit-incomplete`: one character limit per slot kind of a list, measured by filling the frame (`checks.listLimitsDiffering`, `text-fit.json`); template showcase pages (demo-only content, a search results page, picture-only pages in their section) leave the kit and are named in `excludedPages` (`checks.showcasePagesNotExcluded`); page groups follow the real structure, a listing with its own items and the detail pages its list links to, represented by the listing page (`checks.mixedPageGroups`, `checks.detailPagesOutsideListing`, `checks.representativesNotListing`); a slot belongs to the one block that renders it (`checks.slotsInTwoBlocks`); every fixed text has a stable `id` in the planner's `{id, text, canHide}` shape (`checks.fixedTextIdsInvalid`). Short CMS words printed beside a longer string (`Prev`/`Next` of the article navigation) are now found and measured, and a value shared by several language files is named after the template's or the CMS's own constant before an add-on's. A short last row pressed to the left is a template gap only when the content around it is centred; a short row in a left-aligned column, in line with the words beside it, is right. The JA Boxing 1.0.10 contract is re-sealed with author article lists and the footer icon slots, and its approved `template-kit.json` sits beside it.
  **Why / Measured:** on a local stand restored from the 1.0.10 installer the kit reads 50 pages in 12 groups (four template showcase pages left out by structure), 76 blocks and 110 images, 0 blocks without a shot, every self-check at 0 (items lists without card fields, list limits differing, showcase pages kept, mixed page groups, detail pages outside their listing, representatives that are not the listing, slots in two blocks, fixed text ids), 0 open template gaps (31 accepted placeholder links), 168 fixed texts with ids (the article navigation's `Prev`/`Next` now among them, named by Joomla's own constants), and 2 measurement errors left (two lists with no row set of their size at desktop width). Its image set changed, so the kit beside the contract is unapproved until a person approves it again. The template-kit and content-coverage test files pass one file at a time.
