- **The write step's route stage rewrites links still written to an address it renamed; the site check blames such dead links on step 9 (card AD)** — Write step · #415 · `fix/write-step-rewrite-renamed-links` · 2026-11-06
  **Affects:** sites written by the write step (`apps/tracy-web/src/write-step/renamed-link-stage.js`, `src/lib/renamed-addresses.js`): after the kept pages take their plan slugs, every url menu item and every slot of the content contract whose link names a renamed address (or a page under it), in the `/index.php/...` or plain form, is written to the new address through `content.update` (menu item `link`) or `content.contract apply`, and read back. A full address with a host is rewritten only on a host passed as `siteHosts`; otherwise it, and anything the plugin refuses, is a refused or failed line, never silent. The site check's `dead-links` names step 9 for a dead link to a renamed address.
  **Why / Measured:** On a bench site the stage renamed the services page's menu item, and the four footer links (url menu items holding the old `/index.php/<old>/<page>` address) answered 404 on every page while the check blamed the planner.
