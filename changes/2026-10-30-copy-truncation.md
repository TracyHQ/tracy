- **The copy writer is never cut off by its own reasoning, a cut answer is split instead of kept, and every call carries the brief's data.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds and every planner run through `planConcurrently`. The seed model reads a provider's `finish_reason` "length" or "max_tokens" as `TRUNCATED` (thrown at once, never returned, never retried at the same size; counted in `usage().truncated`). The planners' `generate` (`firmGenerate`) asks for the model's own maximum output (`maxTokens: 'max'`, read once from the provider's `GET /models`; 8192 when none is stated) instead of 4096, so reasoning keeps its room. A truncated call is answered `failed: "truncated"`; the Joomla planner splits truncated, failed and unreadable batches up to three times (per page, per section, in halves), and an unreadable answer is retried once before it is split. The WordPress planner splits a truncated or failed batch in halves and records the call's own reason. The copy receipt has six kinds: truncated, unreadable, model-failed, deadline, refused, complaint-unmet; truncated and unreadable lines go to the repair turn, never blanked. The Joomla copy writer receives `brief.factSheet` (offerings with their own words and photo captions, people, reviews, posts, key messages, audience, service areas, primary action, logo reading) read from the content brief (`woContent.briefFor`, `brief-fact-sheet.js`); a catalogue over 24 offerings sends each call its own offerings whole and the others by name. The seeder CLI does not cache a plan with truncated batches. Not yet: the seeder CLI's own chat (`model.mjs`) still reads a cut answer as text, and the WordPress and EmDash writers do not receive the fact sheet.
