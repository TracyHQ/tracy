- **The rebuilding lock is taken as soon as the site is up, and a repair round can make progress** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** brief builds, the repair turns of the site agent, and visitors of a site being built. The lock now finds the brief build from the build's frozen choice (`wo_build.choice.quickstartWorkflow`); it read the template-free brief and was never taken. It is taken the moment the site is up and its door connected, beside the words still being made, with one more try before the first write. The demo-leftover check no longer counts a demo file named only in a stylesheet selector or a CSS comment (Tracy's own hide rule) as a picture on the page. A repair round lists a place found on many pages once, with its pages. The site tools read a coded refusal of the site's own content reader (a structured `error.code`, such as the 503 `CONTENT_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`) as an answer, so the turn is pointed at the reads and writes that still work instead of being told to stop changing the site.
