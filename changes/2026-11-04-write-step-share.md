- **Write step bench, part 13: a share picture for each page** · Claude, for Akemi · #364 · `feat/write-step-share` · 2026-11-04
  **Affects:** whoever runs the write step bench. A new last stage (`share-stage.js`, no phase of its own) turns the template's Open Graph switch on (`system_opengraph` in the params of each template style of the site's template, through `content.update` kind `templateStyle`) and writes `og_img` in the params of the menu item that opens each page the plan keeps, keeping the item's other settings. The picture is the page's top picture: the first block from the top of the page (the kit's 1440 block list) that shows a picture the picture stage reports as written, a body background counting in every frame the kit lists for it. A picture the stage refused, failed to upload or skipped is never named. A page with none gets the brand logo the brand stage wrote to the site profile (light before dark). A page with a picture or logo but no published menu item, or with neither, is reported skipped with the reason. Report lines: `<page>:share-image` and `share-switch:templateStyle-<id>`, each written line with a `readFrom` for the site check. The site-wide `other_shareImage` is not written. No customer-facing change until a run is made on a site.
  **Why / Measured:** ADR 0044 decision 4, item 4. Door found by reading: the quickstart's `ja-boxing/j6/1.0.10` kit says the switch is `system_opengraph` (off) and the per-page setting is a menu entry `og_*` value; its presentation lock shows `og_type`, `og_title`, `og_desc`, `og_img` in the params of its menu items, and a template style of a template that has the switch on holds `"system_opengraph":"1"`. Not measured on a live 1.0.10 site yet: whether `content.update` takes `templateStyle` params, whether the template prints `og:image` when `og_title` is empty, and whether a plain `images/...` path is accepted. Unit tests only, on a fake site door.
