- **EmDash personalize: with the customer's address, no demo street is left on the site; the brief's address also feeds a template's map link** — Hano (EmDash templates) · #(PR number pending) · `feat/emdash-content-map-v3` · 2026-11-17
  **Affects:** every EmDash template (`tools/personalize.mjs` and `tests/personalize-demo-street.test.mjs` in the six `packages/cms/tracy-emdash-theme-*`, and the kit in `vendor/emdash/tracy-emdash-skill`); JA Restaurant 1.0.3 (`seed/personalize.json` writes `chrome.footer_map_url` from `{map}`, keep-demo). A new fact `{map}` (the brief's address as a Google Maps search link) is available to `identity` rules. When the brief gives an address, a last pass takes every sentence that names the demo's place (its proper place names and a numbered street such as "5th Avenue", never a word of the customer's own address) out of every field a `copy`, `rewrite`, `blocks` or `facts` rule owns, with or without a model, and names those fields in the report (`skipped`, rule `places`). With no address, the demo's address and these words stay (keep-demo).
  **Why / Measured:** 17/11/2026, independent review of JA Restaurant 1.0.3: applied with a `contact` answer and no model key, every footer still linked the map to "4 5th Avenue, Brooklyn" and the home welcome and Our History still printed the demo's street; 21 places on 7 pages, the same on 1.0.2. After the fix: 0 of 42 pages on a clean install with the same answers; with no address everything stays. Tests: `personalize-demo-street.test.mjs` 6 of 6 (with and without an address, and with a stub model that answers nothing usable); personalize suites Restaurant 137 and Diner 109 pass; kit-sync 6 of 6; the kit's documented test count is now 407.
  **Second round (independent review of pack.9):** the pass removes only the place phrase now ("Opened in 1982 at 4 5th Avenue" → "Opened in 1982"), a sentence only when nothing readable is left, and a heading, caption or alt left empty takes the page's title or the row's year; the package audit passes on 42 of 42 pages after personalize with an address (it failed on the Our History h1). `identity` rules gain a `rows` form; JA Restaurant's footer contact lines use it, so the demo phone, fax and e-mail never stand beside the brief's phone. `tools/clean-install.sh` keeps `install.log` and `build.log` and says how to finish after a failure; `tools/admin-roundtrip.mjs` writes a top-level `status`.
  **Third round:** a demo place that is a core argument of its sentence (the subject or object of a verb, or a word inside a phrase such as "a Brooklyn favourite") takes its whole sentence; only a detachable place phrase (a preposition and the place, or the place set off by commas or brackets) goes alone, and no word is invented in its place. JA Restaurant's home welcome no longer reads "has welcomed to one of the neighborhood's oldest buildings": its first sentence goes and the paragraph keeps the next one.
  **Fourth round (third independent review of JA Restaurant 1.0.3, owner hard rule):** a phone in the brief replaces the demo's phone in every field of every page, with or without an address, found by its digits in any spelling and in `tel:` links (a new lane `contacts`); the demo's web address and e-mail go beside it, and the brief's `email` replaces the demo's. A last check scans every field and fails the run (`contacts-left`, exit 4) when the demo phone or e-mail domain is left while the brief has the matching value. The `places` report line names only fields that held the demo's place, with `changes` before and after. Kit test count 409.
  **Fifth round (code review, 2026-11-18):** a place word inside the customer's own values (the name, the contact lines, the e-mail, the hours, the description) is never the demo's place, so a customer named "Brooklyn Bistro" keeps "Welcome to Brooklyn Bistro"; a heading, caption or alt text is never emptied (year, title, then the brief's name, else it keeps its words and is reported); the demo's web domain matches only as a whole host or a subdomain, so another site such as "notres.example" keeps its sentence. Kit test count 412.
  **Sixth round (independent review of JA Diner 0.1.3, owner decisions of 2026-11-18):** personalize never leaves a field empty that held text (a short field takes the heading fallback, any other keeps its words and is reported); a place is a name ("Mountain View" is one place, "View" alone is none, an address's country is none); the place pass touches only the fields its rules name and never button or link labels; an article's title never becomes the brand; the template vendor's domain (`vendor.domains` in `template.config.json`) is never a demo domain, so a credit such as "Designed by JoomlArt.com." stays; `identity` row rules take any row key and fields. Kit test count 417.
  **Seventh round (code review of the sixth):** the earlier pass over rows a model left as the template's follows the same rules (no emptied field, no button or link label); a country is any name of a real list of countries (ISO 3166 names in English, plus Britain, UK, USA and a few others), wherever it stands in an address; a kept text is reported by its path inside its field.
