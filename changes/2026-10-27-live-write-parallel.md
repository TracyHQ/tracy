- **Sync to live writes independent items side by side** — David (Claude) · #PR · `perf/live-sync-latency-3` · 2026-10-27
  **Affects:** owners of imported sites pressing Send or Undo with more than a few items. `lib/live-write.mjs` writes the items it sends alone (each a read, a write and a read back) up to `TRACY_LIVE_WRITE_CONCURRENCY` (4) at once instead of one after the other: an item starts once its dependencies ended, one lane (a live record with its meta, a WordPress menu, the terms, the options, a Joomla tree, the doors of their own) is written one item at a time in order, and a live site that answers busy even once is written one item at a time from then on. The journal, `before.json` and the answer (in the changeset's order) are those of one at a time; eight slow WordPress posts took under 60% of the time in the test. Joomla's `content.batch` lots are unchanged.
  **Deploy debt:** the fleet scripts ship in the `tracy-host` image, rebuilt on merge to `develop`; production with the next `develop` to `main` release. `TRACY_LIVE_WRITE_CONCURRENCY=1` in the host's environment is the old behaviour.
