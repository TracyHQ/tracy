- **Quickstart builds label the brief's photos in picture preparation, never while the site write clock runs.** — Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** Quickstart-first builds on every CMS. The vision labelling of the brief's photos moved from Apply into the engine's picture preparation, under the drawing budget; each label is kept on its photo as before. A vision batch starts only while the budget can hold it and is never cut mid-call (a cut paid attempt would block the run's later calls). Apply reads the kept labels with `forSite({label: false})`, which asks no model and hands out no drawing check; photos still unlabelled are placed by their words and counted in the fill receipt (`photoLabels`) and a build note. A quickstart build with uncached labels no longer records `PICTURE_MODEL_DURING_APPLY`. The demo look and the menu photo reading are proven outside every module a brief build loads.
