- **The picture step cuts the picker's photos four at a time and each once, and few frames on the any-photo rung look further ahead** — David (WO pipeline) · #639 · `perf/wo-picture-prep` · 2026-11-16
  **Affects:** Rebuild and deploy tracy-web. Chain builds and picture bench runs: the code half's picker marks, the picker's shortlists and its thumbnails are cut `CUTS_AT_ONCE` (4) at a time, and one `cutMemo` per preparation (shared by `fillFrames` `pickerCut` and `pickerInput` `cut`) makes each (photo, picker frame) cut once. On the any-photo rung each waiting frame has `aheadDepth` next photos looked at together: `LOOK_AHEAD` (3) with many frames, up to `LOOK_CONCURRENCY` (8) with one. Picks, cuts, ladder and verdicts are unchanged; a photo looked at ahead and never placed costs its look.
  **Why / Measured:** Dev, 16/11/2026: the picker's input took 49 s (Arabian Tea House) and 83 s (BXR London) with sharp on one thread (its glibc default), and BXR London's first fix round spent 5.2 minutes on 77 looks; pictures were 23.0 minutes of a 47.8-minute build. Against develop's code with sharp on one thread: identical output, 200 photos and 28 picker frames 158.7 s → 41.7 s. Case REG-PR-639-picture-prep-parallel; every site-pictures test file with a tmpfs Postgres 265 pass.
