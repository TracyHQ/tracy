- **Website Opportunities brief readiness gate: every new brief revision is measured, a blocked one starts no build** — Akemi (Claude) · `feat/brief-readiness-labels` · 2026-10-28
  **Affects:** everyone who starts demo builds, and anyone reading `wo_revision.brief` or `wo_build.receipt`. `domain/brief-readiness.mjs` measures a brief beside its validation (own usable photos 8/4, labelled share 90%/60%, source and credit 100%, hero candidates 2/1, offerings with a linked photo 60%/30% and with a description 80%/50%, named people with a portrait, items with a role or reason, template content kinds) into checks, a verdict (pass, warn, block) and owner questions; `createRevision` stores it as `brief.readiness`. `startBuild` refuses a blocked revision with 409 `BRIEF_NOT_READY` (the demo action stops with that error, and the client-send demo claims no site); a warned one builds and `receipt.readiness` carries the owner questions. A revision made before this carries no readiness and builds as before. Each demo QA round now keeps `readiness`: leftover template sample text and collected items shown on the live site, with the brief's checks carried as measured on the brief. The template content kinds check stays `unknown` until a build's template states the kinds of cards it shows.
