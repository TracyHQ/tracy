- **The look guard no longer flags a role written over social icons for a person with no social account** — Write step bench (card AB) · #pending · `fix/site-check-zero-checked` · 2026-11-06
  **Affects:** step 9 look guard (`write-step/look-guard-social.js`) and the step 11 look-guard-findings group that reads it. A cell whose demo content is only social icon links, written for a subject the brief gives no social account, may take words in their place; the lost icon classes go to `report.guard.allowed` under the named rule `social-icons-without-accounts` instead of the findings. Any other finding stays. ADR 0044 decision 10.
  **Why / Measured:** The product owner accepted the icons going when there is no account to link. On a bench run the four coach role cells gave 28 blocking lines every run; replaying the guard offline on that run's files gives 0 findings and 28 allowed. Tested with invented people and accounts.
