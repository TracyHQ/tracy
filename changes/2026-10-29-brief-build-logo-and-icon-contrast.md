- **A brief build's logo and item icons stay readable on the ground they are shown on** - Akemi (Claude) · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** Joomla brief builds. A logo whose marks do not reach 3:1 against a dark (or light) logo spot is set on a plate of the colour that contrasts with the logo, on the spots of that side only (a single logo becomes a pair); a plate never makes a too-small logo pass the slot's legibility check. A drawn item icon takes the demo icon's ink: when the demo icon carries its own ground (a disc or tile behind a line glyph), the glyph colour is the one that stands apart from that ground, held to 3:1, not the average of the whole picture. The footer logo placed through the module HTML drops the demo's `data-path` and fixed `width`.
  **Why / Measured:** on a local brief build on a Joomla T4 template a dark green badge logo sat on the dark header and footer, and the drawn feature icons came out near black (the demo icons are red strokes on a black disc) on a dark band.
