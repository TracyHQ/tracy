- **The fill plan fills person slots with the named person's portrait or an initials avatar, ranks the owner's photos first, keeps map photos by others out of identity slots, and crops around the subject without cutting a face.** — Akemi (COO), with Claude Code · `feat/fill-plan-photo-rules` · 2026-10-29
  **Affects:** Joomla builds from a version 3 content map (`planFill`, `shapeReal`). A person card takes only the portrait of the person its name says, else an initials avatar (`initialsAvatar` in `apps/tracy-web/src/lib/picture-cut.js`, receipt kind `avatar`, counted as a placeholder). The hero, an `editorial.illustration` picture and a gallery's lead picture refuse a photo someone else posted on a map listing. Photo crops now come from `subjectCrop` (strategy `subject` with an `at` share, cut by `cutForPlace`); refusal codes `crop-loss`, `too-small-after-crop`, `cuts-a-face`, `map-photo-by-others`, `portrait-of-another`, `face-not-linked` and `not-a-portrait` replace the old `shape`. The slot minimum is the Slot fit `minWidth` when the map declares it, so a hero of JA Boxing 1.0.8 asks for a larger photo than before.
