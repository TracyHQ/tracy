- **Audit tooling: sticky elements measure at their resting place, and the content map verify viewer ships** - Akemi (Claude) · `feat/template-readiness-rules` · 2026-10-28
  **Affects:** whoever runs the content coverage audit (`--fit`) or cuts a template contract. The audit's lazy-content wake now switches smooth scrolling off and settles at the top before it reads boxes, so a sticky or fixed header no longer lands at a different place on each run on pages that set `scroll-behavior: smooth` (the Slot fit boxes of such elements were wrong). New tool `content-coverage/verify-viewer.mjs` renders each component's screenshot with numbered slot outlines beside its content-map values and lists slots that were not located or not shown; the `quickstart-contract` skill runs it after the coverage step.
  **Why / Measured:** on a fixture page with a smooth-scrolling sticky header the box came out at y=2899 before the fix and at the top of the page after it, with three runs identical (`content-coverage-sticky.test.mjs`); the viewer's locating is covered by `content-map-verify-viewer.test.mjs`.
