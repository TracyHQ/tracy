- **A Joomla brief build writes its copy from one site direction, and every specific fact a copy writer states names its source in the brief.** — build maintainers · #189 · `feat/brief-build-own-site` · 2026-10-30
  **Affects:** Joomla brief builds that have a content brief (`woContent.briefFor`). One site-direction call (`site-direction.js`) reads the whole fact digest and the pages the plan writes before the page writers, and answers a voice, a lead per page and the sourceIds of the facts that belong on each page; the page writer receives it as `brief.direction`, each batch its own pages as `pageDirection` (time recorded as `siteDirectionMs`; a failed call writes without a direction). Every record of `brief.factSheet` now carries a `sourceId` (`offering-n`, `person-n`, `review-n`, `post-n`, `message-n`, `story-n`, `photo-n`; photo labels are `{sourceId, label}`, story lines `{sourceId, text}`); the brief's own fields are sources by their name and statistics are `statistic-n` (`fact-sources.mjs` of the seeder, a new seeder module). Writers answer `sources` beside their words. A line that states a specific fact (a number, price, date, phone, email, web address, a person's name from the brief, or a claim such as an award, certification, licence or guarantee) that no source it cites states is sent back once, then refused: in the page writer as pending reason `unsourced-fact` (receipt kind `refused`), in the later writers (offering descriptions, sibling lists, record pages, call-to-action bands, through `copy-sources.js` `sourcedWriter`) dropped from the answer and recorded as `copyUnsourcedRefused`. Explanatory copy with no specific fact needs no source. The plan carries `sources` by key. Debt: the WordPress and EmDash writers get neither the direction nor sourced facts yet; the box rewrite after a refused write (`refused-box-rewrite.js`, apply step) only shortens an already sourced line and is not wrapped; specific-fact detection is English for claims and does not see facts written as words ("twelve years").
