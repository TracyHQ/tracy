JA Restaurant: the JoomlArt Restaurant design as a WordPress block theme, carried from its Joomla 6 (T3) quickstart 1.0.6.

== Description ==

JA Restaurant is the RES Bar & Restaurant site packaged as a block theme, ported from the JoomlArt JA Restaurant Joomla 6 quickstart (T3 framework) so that it renders the same pages: the slideshow, the welcome and story sections, the special offers, the menu in tabs, the news, the gallery, the happy hours, the team and the history timeline. Every section of the site is a block pattern the Site Editor can edit; the header carries the opening hours, the logo, the main menu with its dropdowns, a light / dark / automatic switch that follows the operating system until the visitor chooses, and on phones a button that opens the menu in a side panel.

The source has no dark mode: the dark colours are derived from its palette. The newsletter box draws the source's field and button; WordPress core keeps no mailing list, so it needs a newsletter plugin to collect addresses. The contact form needs a form plugin to deliver messages.

This theme is not intended for the wordpress.org directory: it bundles patterns for a specific site. It does not update itself; a newer version is installed by uploading its zip.
