# JA Restaurant 1.0.2: visual review of the Tracy acceptance run (D65)

The first full-scope acceptance run on the 1.0.2 pack (31 lens runs, every tile opened) raised **161 notes** (light desktop 49, light responsive 84,
dark desktop 8, dark responsive 20; 16 flagged LEE). Each was classed against the source mirror and the recorded decisions:

- **fixed**: a real difference, fixed at its cause in shared CSS, a view or the seed (74 notes, grouped by cause in D65);
- **decided gap**: exactly one of the differences recorded in `inventory/content-gaps.md` (G-14/G-17 Most Read, G-18 icon glyphs, G-19 map, G-20 filter bars,
  G-21 parallax photographs) (66 notes): closed with the G number, nothing else changed;
- **matches source**: the source shows the same wrap, gap or widow at that size, measured or checked on the source tile (21 notes).

After the fixes two more full runs were made with fresh lens agents (round 1: 154 notes, round 2: 126 notes; the lens readers do not converge, see
`references/qa-lenses.md` §8 of the kit). Round 2's open notes are the decided gaps and the D33 footer credits plus a handful of measurable differences
listed at the end of D65; the review gate was **not** passed.
