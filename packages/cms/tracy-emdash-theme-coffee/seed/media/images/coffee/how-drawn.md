# How the demo pictures were drawn

Every picture in this folder was drawn by Codex (the built-in image tool of codex-cli 0.162.1, `codex exec`) on 2026-10-30, then shrunk to WebP (cwebp -q 78, 1200 px wide, portraits 800 px). No stock photo, no copied media. Style tail added to every prompt:

> Moody editorial coffee-shop photography in a warm dark palette: deep espresso-brown and dark walnut tones, soft warm rim light, cream, kraft-paper and matte ceramic accents, shallow depth of field, premium and cosy, natural and realistic. Landscape 4:3. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no signs, no readable labels anywhere in the picture. Any takeaway paper cup must be plain, blank and unprinted. Do not invent a style beyond this.
