# Site content profiles

`tracy-editorial/content-contract.json` is the business/copy authority for the working studio demo.
It follows schemaVersion 1 of the JoomlArt T4 skill's
[content contract](../../../../vendor/joomla/joomlart-t4-skill/skills/joomlart-t4-template/references/content-contract.md).
This is separate from `../contracts/`, which protects a published quickstart's CMS structure.

The profile covers 128 existing route paths plus 12 marketing artifact theme variants. Facts
record the fictional studio, six services, four people, three illustrative plans, process and CTA.
Source notes identify the live pages read on 2026-09-12. Contact recipient, real-business prices,
testimonials, customer logos and performance statistics are not approved facts. Existing image
paths identify demo assets; they do not establish permission to reuse a portrait as a real person.
Review image rights before customer use. The profile is public-demo data, not customer evidence.

Home and Onepage builders read shared facts through `studio-content.mjs`. Their remaining
editorial copy is kept in the existing builders/copy map. This migration does not assert that
all existing live pages comply: their complete field plans and DOM observations are still needed.

`tracy-editorial/content-plan.json` covers all 140 surfaces and is generated by
`../../build-content-plan.mjs`: the twelve artifact surfaces from the field sets in
`../../marketing-plan.mjs`, where a light and a dark artifact share one field set and cannot drift
apart, and the 128 site surfaces from `../../site-plan.mjs`.

The two groups are read back differently because they are different things. Artifacts are rendered
by this repo and carry build-time `data-field` markers, so `../../observe-marketing-samples.mjs`
reads them inside the samples page's sandboxed iframe. Site pages are Joomla views with no markers,
so `../../site-plan.mjs` declares a CSS selector per field and `../../observe-site-pages.mjs` drives
Chrome across every URL; it exits non-zero when a selector matches nothing, because a scanner that
reports success after seeing nothing is worse than no scanner. `../../audit-content.mjs` merges both
observations and audits at the contract's full scope.

`tracy-editorial/site-copy.json` holds the copy those 128 pages render today, with every business fact
written back as a `{{reference}}`, so a heading stating a person, a service or an amount resolves
through the contract rather than freezing a literal that outlives it. Substitution is deliberately
conservative: plan names such as Studio and Starter are also ordinary words on this site, so they
stay literal. Most site surfaces are audited at title, heading and disclosure; the six that declare
`requiredFacts` are read fact by fact.

Keep this profile when changing the Apple visual inspiration; a new business requires a separate
profile, not an unrelated sample brand overwriting these facts.

From the repository root:

```bash
node vendor/joomla/joomlart-t4-skill/skills/joomlart-t4-template/scripts/content-contract.mjs validate packages/cms/tracy-joomla-quickstart/content/tracy-editorial/content-contract.json
```

Use `compileContentPlan(contract, plan, surfaceIds)` for a scoped migration, with the exact agreed
surface IDs from this profile. The default CLI `compile` and `audit` require the whole profile.
A changed hash invalidates earlier plans and observations. Preserve revision/history and evidence;
do not make the profile smaller merely to pass validation. For deployment, the Joomla content/display
Claude Cowork plugin remains a separate guard. No claim is made that the current site is bound to archive 1.1.0.
