# 0033 — Talking by voice: chaining STT → agent → TTS

**Status:** (binding) · 16/10/2026 · David requested it and settled the points along the way; Claude recorded. The council (gpt-6.1-sol) timed out twice (20 minutes), David allowed going ahead without the council.

## Context

Voice input already exists (#1364, #1366, #1386): a microphone next to Send, text goes into the compose box, the customer sends it themselves. David wants a feature like ChatGPT's voice mode. The customer speaks, Tracy works and then answers aloud, with an icon for recording and for responding, and the recognized text shows in the Chat tab. Along the way David also settled:

- no silence while the agent works;
- the customer speaking while the agent works is handled like a follow-up chat message;
- turning voice on disallows typing;
- shown according to the Voice switch in `/ops`.

It differs from ChatGPT in one point: one turn of the Tracy agent runs tools and edits a real site, sometimes for several minutes. The final answer is markdown, meant to be read by eye.

## Decision

1. **Chain three steps, no speech-to-speech model.**
   - The customer's words go through the existing STT relay (`relay.js`, ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime), then into the dsh agent as an ordinary message.
   - Spoken output goes through a new door `POST /api/speech/say` in tracy-web (ElevenLabs Flash v2.5, PCM 24 kHz streamed to the page).
   - The realtime "receptionist" option (OpenAI Realtime, ElevenLabs Agents) calling the agent through a tool was dropped: only the agent knows the site, so a fast-talking model without the facts would make things up (CLAUDE.md principle 3).
   - Flash v2.5 chosen over Turbo v2.5 based on measurements of 16/10: first audio out after 195 ms vs. 328 ms (median of 5 runs, 133 Vietnamese characters). Transcribed back with Scribe, both are equally clear (6.5% word error).
2. **Relay, no token issued to the browser** (same reason as #1364). The key stays on the server, characters are counted before calling the service:
   - 600 characters per read-out;
   - 20,000 characters/day/account, 300,000 characters/day for the whole machine (`rate_bucket`, `overLimitBy`);
   - if counting fails, refuse;
   - if the service refuses, give the characters back.
3. **One voice switch** (`composer-voice-input`, `/ops` › Agents › Voice). The microphone and the conversation are switched on and off together. A separate switch, off by default (the first version), was dropped at David's request.
4. **Send like a typed message.**
   - A settled sentence plus 0.3 s with no new words is sent through `conversation.sendSession` (1.6 s until 17/10/2026, see item 13).
   - If Tracy is idle it is a new message (`'queue'`). If Tracy is working, the words are **steered** into the work in progress (`'steer'`, item 11), like a consultant who hears the customer's correction and acts on it right away; if the turn no longer accepts it, dsh queues it on its own.
   - The compose box is not touched.
   - Before sending, the voice row shows the text with a Send now and a Say it again button. Mishearing one word means editing the site wrongly.
5. **The ear opens an STT session when it hears sound on the machine.** Two 100 ms frames above the relay's RMS threshold open a session; one second of audio before that is replayed. Silence is not sent, so it costs no quota (quota is counted by seconds of audio, item 13). A session that yields no words within 6 s is closed, unless the customer is still speaking; a session with words is kept 12 s longer for the next sentence.
6. **Follow every turn that starts after voice is turned on**, including typed messages, plus the turn running at the moment it is turned on. Read from the session's event window (`turn/start`, `assistant/message`, `tool/call`, `tool/result`, `turn/end`, with `data.turn`). History loaded later is never read out.
7. **Say what is being done, don't make things up.** At most 3 sentences per turn (8 sentences until 17/10/2026; council: eight sentences made Tracy talk almost the whole time it worked, and customers hesitated to cut in), at least 6 s apart. Sentences are taken in this order:
   - the first sentence of what the agent wrote before a tool call;
   - if there is none, a pre-translated sentence by kind of work (looking at the site, editing text, changing the look, images, making a change, putting back as before);
   - the question of a question card.

   Tool names are never read out. Final answer: read the opening part (item 14), at most 45 words counted in whole sentences (`SPOKEN_WORDS`; on a real site the agent once opened with 60 words although the rule says ≤ 40, so the limit lives in code, not just in the prompt), drop markdown, code and addresses, cut at a sentence boundary, say "The rest is in the chat", then read the final question if the answer ends with a question. A failed turn where the agent wrote nothing says "There was an error, so it stopped. Details are in the chat."; a turn the customer stopped themselves stays silent (dsh records the turn's end reason as `{ kind }`).
8. **The customer can interrupt, and speaker sound is not mistaken for the customer** (David 16/10/2026: "when I keep talking, the agent breaks until I finish talking, then the agent answers"; replaces the approach in #1406 where the ear closed while Tracy spoke).
   - The ear stays open while Tracy speaks. Sound louder than Tracy's echo (RMS 1200, 3 frames of 100 ms; the browser's echo cancellation already removes most of it) opens an STT session and turns Tracy down at once (25%).
   - If the first words are not Tracy's sentence, and not just "yeah/yes" (item 11), Tracy stops, listens to the customer finish the sentence, then answers that sentence. After 2 s with no words from the customer, Tracy turns back up and carries on.
   - Heard words that are mostly one contiguous piece of the sentence read in the last 4 s are dropped; a contiguous piece of 3 or more words at the start of the sentence is cut off. This applies only to sound starting while Tracy is speaking or within 0.5 s after: "Green" said after Tracy reads "Red, Green or Yellow" is the customer choosing, not an echo (council 17/10/2026).
   - Tracy stops only on two or more words, or one stop word ("khoan", "stop", "停"); an unclear sound ("Dzuiii.", "Du. 2." on the stand) does not. While a question card is being read, one word is enough.
   - Interrupting while work is being narrated does not silence Tracy for the whole turn; only pressing stop-speaking does that.
9. **Chat or Voice for each chat, like the ChatGPT app** (David 16/10/2026, #1409; replaces the approach in #1406 of talking right in the Chat view with a voice row and a locked compose box).
   - Voice is its own `conversation.view` (`tracy-voice`). dsh does not draw its tab; the Chat/Voice menu in the header and the sound-wave button in the chat box select it. dsh has no API to select a view, so Tracy clicks that very tab button, like the Comments view.
   - In Voice, the chat body is the orb with the heard words or the sentence being spoken. The chat box is replaced by a bottom bar (mute mic, X) through the `conversation.composer` chain, so typing is not possible. dsh's question cards and approval requests still show in place of the bottom bar.
   - Voice is tied to the view: leaving the view (X, Chat, another tab, another chat) ends it. A view that dsh remembers and reopens after a page reload has no voice yet; the customer taps the orb to start, because the browser only allows opening the microphone and speaker from a tap.
   - A new chat has no header yet, so Voice is entered through the sound-wave button. The Voice view declares itself "active" when the customer wants in (the Comments view's approach), so a new chat gets a tab bar.
10. **Web first** (ADR 0030): all of it is pieces of the web page. The desktop app renders the same page and already has the microphone entitlement.
11. **Turn-taking like a real consultant** (David 16/10/2026: "check carefully… handle it like the behavior of a real consultant"). The words for all 8 interface languages live in `voice-turn-taking.mjs`; only a sentence made *entirely* of those words counts:
    - **Still thinking, so wait.** A sentence ending in "và…", "nhưng…", "and…", a comma or a hesitation sound waits 2 s instead of 0.3 s. A question mark does not make the sentence go sooner: punctuation is the STT's, not the customer's (council 17/10/2026).
    - **While the customer is still speaking, don't send and don't cut in.** If the microphone had sound in the last 0.8 s, a sentence that is due still waits for words (up to 4 s more), and Tracy does not start a new sentence. Measured on the stand 16/10: the first words of the next sentence arrived 2.5 s after the customer started speaking, so relying on words alone cut the sentence in two.
    - **"Um" is never a message.** "Ừ", "vâng", "okay" said while Tracy is speaking, or when Tracy is not waiting for any answer, means the customer is listening: Tracy carries on, nothing is sent. "Dạ vâng" before a request is also dropped from the message (on the stand, the STT also heard a sentence "Dạ vâng ạ." in the silence before a request). After Tracy asks (the answer has a question mark), "ok" is an answer and is sent. While a question card is open, every utterance, including "ừ" and a single word, is an answer to the card (council 17/10/2026); "ừ" means "yes", not a hesitation sound ("ừm" is).
    - **Hesitation sounds, however they are written.** The STT wrote "ừ ừ" as "Uuuh." on the stand; Tracy stopped and the agent took it as the customer being impatient. So drawn-out sounds ("uuuh", "hmmm", "ừừ", "ähm") are also hesitation sounds; "ạ", "em", "ô" are not, because they are Vietnamese words.
    - **A correction while working is acted on at once** (steer, item 4), and Tracy says "Heard you. Will do that right away." On real dsh, the correction shows in the chat as a steer message, and the agent acts on it right in that turn.
    - **One "heard you" sentence for what was just said**, said before any narration sentence; if the customer says two things in a row, the later sentence replaces the earlier one not yet spoken.
    - **Ask aloud, hear the answer aloud.** A question card is read with its options ("Red, Green or Yellow", `Intl.ListFormat`). The customer's next sentence answers that very card through dsh's verb (`PendingQuestion.answer`, read from `uiSession.sessionStatus` like `tracy-ask-form`): it picks the named option if that is all the customer said, otherwise it keeps the customer's words as a free-form answer. Sent as a message, those words would sit waiting behind a turn that is waiting on that very card.
    - **No silence during long work.** If Tracy is working and has said nothing for 15 s, it says "Still working." (still within the limit of 3 sentences per turn, and not after the customer pressed stop-speaking). This sentence and the confirmation sentence have two phrasings, rotated.
    - **A question left hanging is asked again once.** If Tracy asks (a question card, or an answer ending with a question mark) and there is no sound for 12 s, it asks again exactly once and then waits; silence is never consent (council 17/10/2026).
    - **Flaky network.** If the STT session drops because of the network once, Tracy keeps listening ("Lost the connection for a moment. Listening again."); twice within 30 s, it pauses. This is a deliberate exception to the "no retry" convention: a root fix cannot cure weak Wi-Fi (council 17/10/2026).
    - **Background noise and laughter.** The speech threshold follows the room's background noise (2.5 times the level of the quietest tenth of frames over 5 s, while Tracy is not speaking; not lower than the old threshold, not higher than 2500); a steadily humming fan no longer keeps the STT session open. "Haha", "hì", "哈哈" and the "(laughs)" label do not become messages.
    - **Stop the work by voice or by button.** "Dừng lại", "khoan đã", "đợi chút", "stop", "wait", "停下来" said on their own (`isStopCommand`) while Tracy is working stop the turn entirely, like dsh's Stop button. With more content ("khoan đã, để màu đỏ" — "hold on, make it red") it is a correction and is steered. Talking over Tracy while it reads the answer only makes Tracy stop talking. A bare stop command never becomes a message (council 17/10/2026, two rounds: a shop owner says "khoan" to stop a change). Voice's bottom bar takes the place of the chat box and its Stop button, so it has its own stop-work button while Tracy is working (dsh's `session.cancel()`). Tracy says "Stopped. What was already saved stays as it is." (no undo).
12. **A small note introducing Voice** on the site's start screen (empty session), with an arrow pointing at the sound-wave button: "Use your voice with Tracy here".
    - Clicking the note goes straight into Voice: it clicks the sound-wave button itself, so Voice starts only along one path (David 17/10/2026).
    - Closing it (X, clicking the note, clicking the sound-wave button, or choosing Voice) closes it until the page is reloaded; it is stored nowhere.
    - Not shown to viewers, during onboarding, or when voice is off.
    - The sound-wave button no longer has a hover tooltip: the note already says it (David 17/10/2026, "turn that tip off, I don't think it's needed").
    - When the chat already has a header, the note sits under the Chat/Voice button, with the arrow pointing at that button (David 17/10/2026, "tip here"); clicking it goes into Voice. The note moves out of the way when the menu opens.
    - The mute button changes icon with its state: a microphone while listening, a crossed-out microphone when muted (David 17/10/2026).
13. **As fast as a conversation** (council 17/10/2026; references: LiveKit waits 0.5 s, Vapi 0.4 s, AssemblyAI at least 0.4 s). Measured on the stand 16/10 before the fix: from the customer finishing speaking to Tracy's first sound was 3.5–4.2 s, made up of ElevenLabs' VAD settling the sentence after ~1.2 s, Tracy waiting another 1.6 s, sending and playback ~0.7 s.
    - A Voice session asks ElevenLabs to settle a sentence after 0.8 s of silence (`?turns=voice` → `vad_silence_threshold_secs=0.8`; the 1.5 s default is kept for dictation, where the customer pauses while composing).
    - Tracy waits only 0.3 s more; 2 s for an unfinished sentence; if the customer is still making sound it keeps waiting (item 11).
    - The two confirmation sentences ("Got it, doing it now.", "Heard you. Will do that right away.") are synthesized in advance when Voice starts, so they play as soon as the words are sent.
    - The STT quota counts **seconds of audio** received (bucket `speech-seconds*`, deducting 5 s at a time while the session runs, the remainder on close), no longer a round minute per session: on the stand, 12 sessions with 105 s of real speech had been charged 12 minutes, and Voice opens one session per sentence, so about 60 sentences used up the 60 minutes/day. `/ops` › Voice shows minutes converted from seconds.
14. **The agent knows the customer is speaking** (council 17/10/2026 chose per-turn metadata on the host, not a hidden block stuffed into the message, so the history after Voice is exactly like typing). Right before sending a spoken sentence, the page reports that very sentence `{ sessionId, spoken }` through the existing `/view` door of `tracy-site-tools` (the seat gateway already binds this door's `sessionId` to the right seat) and waits for the host to reply (at most 1.5 s; past that the sentence still goes, just without the rule). The step that receives a message with exactly those words gets the rule, once (`voice-reply.mjs` `spokenWords`). The rule is attached to the message itself, not to the conversation (review and council 18/10/2026): before, the page reported `{ sessionId, voice }` every minute and the host kept a per-conversation flag, so messages typed in another tab or on another machine also got the rule, and another tab leaving Voice wiped the rule for the tab that was speaking. The council chose metadata travelling with the message; dsh has no place for metadata on a message yet (`sendSession(session, text, attachments, mode)`), so the message's own text is where it is attached, which also follows exactly the message the step consumes. A steered sentence gets the rule at the step that receives it. Every sentence the customer speaks in Voice gets an extra per-turn rule `tracy-site-tools:voice-reply`: words coming from speech recognition may mishear names, numbers, prices, so a value that does not fit the site is asked about, not guessed, and the value used is said back; before a change that is hard to undo or broad (deleting a page, post, block; changing a price or many items at once), say clearly what will change and wait for "yes"; open with one or two sentences ≤ 40 words, saying the result, what is not done yet and what is needed from the customer; write for listening (no markdown, lists, links, symbols; numbers, prices, dates written out in words, because Flash v2.5 does not normalize numbers); a blank line, then details for the chat. The page reads that opening part aloud.

15. **Know before the customer does that a key is broken** (council 17/10/2026; that night the local machine's key was changed to a key lacking STT and TTS permission, every session and every read-out was refused, and `/ops` said nothing). tracy-web keeps a per-process record (`lib/speech-health.js`), fed by three paths:
    - test the key every 10 minutes while voice is on, and right when the key is saved on `/ops` (`speech/health.js`): STT by opening a realtime session and closing it, sending no audio; TTS by reading one character;
    - every relay session opened (`session_started` means OK, `auth_error` means refused);
    - every read-out (200 means OK; on error, go by the service's words).

    The service's words decide (`detail.status`, `message_type`), not the HTTP code (review 18/10/2026: testing a fake key, TTS returns **400** `invalid_api_key`, not 401, so the earlier version never noticed a wrong TTS key): a refused key or missing permission is `refused`; out of credit, unusual activity, payment required is `limited` — `/ops` shows it, hides nothing, and a one-character test that passes does not clear a limit a real read-out hit within 10 minutes; service busy, too many concurrent requests, network error or a code without the service's words says nothing about the key. Each key save is a new generation: results of a test, session or read-out using the old key are discarded, and the new key is tested right after the test in progress. If STT is refused, `GET /api/speech` returns `available: false` (the page draws no microphone, no Voice); if TTS is refused, `speak` is dropped (no Voice, dictation remains). `/ops` › Voice shows the key's state. Measured 17/10: real key ok/ok; fake key `auth_error`/`invalid_api_key`.
16. **Service text that never settles** (measured on a real site 17/10/2026). The service settles a sentence, then 200 ms later sends a slightly different re-transcription as in-progress text, and never settles that; the page showed the sentence twice and kept it until the session closed for silence, 30 s later. The relay does not show the page the re-transcription, but only when there has been **no new speech** since the sentence just settled (review and council 18/10/2026: comparing text alone, "delete the third image" right after "Delete the second image." was also treated as a re-transcription; if the customer said nothing more there cannot be a new sentence). Not showing it is not the same as done: that text still counts as pending, so on stop the relay still asks the service to settle; if the service settles exactly that re-transcription, nothing is added either. On Done/Enter (dictation) or when the page asks to settle, the relay closes immediately only if no audio has been sent since the last settled sentence; otherwise it asks to settle and waits (measured 18/10/2026: the service answers the settle command after 0.35–0.6 s, an empty string when there is nothing). If 3 s pass without settling, it ends with `settled: false`: Enter does not auto-send a truncated request, the text stays in the box. In Voice, if there is in-progress text and the customer has been silent for 3 s (`STUCK_WORDS_MS`), the page asks the service to settle rather than taking the shown text as a finished sentence; if the service cannot settle, the text stays waiting for "Send now"/"Say it again". In the same runs, two more fixes: pressing stop-work while the customer is still speaking or a sentence is still counting down used to auto-send those words after the stop, and sent when Tracy was already idle, they became new work; now words that started before stop was pressed stay on screen, waiting for "Send now" or "Say it again", Tracy still says "Stopped" at once, and words starting after that go as usual. The held words are kept alive separately from the STT session (review 18/10/2026: before, they were lost after 12 s of silence or when the service closed the session): the session closes, the words remain, no timer deletes them; a new settled sentence that is a request takes their place and goes as usual, while a cough or the first words of the next sentence do not (measured on a real site: pressing stop mid-sentence, the words were not sent; words fully sent before pressing are a steer of the stopped turn, the chat still shows them and dsh does not continue, as with typing); an option ending in "(Recommended)" was read out as literal English, and the customer saying "Đỏ" ("Red") could not pick "Đỏ (Recommended)" (now read and compared as the card shows it, sending the answer with the exact original label, following dsh's `parseRecommendedLabel`).
17. **Code review of 18/10/2026, fixed together with the council** (`tasks/evidence/voice-review-2026-10-18/`):
    - each question card is read once per the call that asked it (`callId`): a second card in the same turn used to go unread, Tracy stayed silent while the Voice screen covered the card; a card's question that was answered before it could be read is no longer read; a question arriving while another sentence is being read is asked as soon as that one finishes, without waiting 6 s;
    - a card with a "Huỷ"/"Cancel"/"取消" option: saying exactly that word (only that word, exact match) picks it, it does not cancel the whole turn; "dừng lại" ("stop") matching no option still stops the work; the Stop button always stops the work;
    - talking over a progress sentence no longer loses the answer queued behind it (and the question in it); talking over the answer itself leaves the rest in the chat; saying "stop" while Tracy is idle drops the whole sentence waiting to be read. The order is kept as before: the answer waiting to be read first, then the "got it" for the new words, so the customer does not think the old sentence is the result of the new words;
    - a plan awaiting approval (`exit_plan_mode`, dsh `plan-review`; every preset has the planning group): Tracy says "There's a plan for you to review on screen", steers no words into the blocked turn, then returns to Chat, where the approval card is; only done when dsh confirms it is awaiting approval, not inferred from the call's name;
    - "thôi", "thôi thôi" ("enough") on their own make Tracy stop reading (previously filtered out as filler); "ok thôi" is listening; "thôi, đổi sang đỏ" ("never mind, change it to red") is a correction. "对对对", "好好好", "是的是的" repeated in a run of words is listening;
    - Chinese echo is compared per Han character (a whole sentence is one "word", so it was never recognized); four Han characters count as two words, so "红色" said back is still the customer choosing;
    - Safari before 17 (and Chrome < 113, Firefox < 112) has no `URLSearchParams.size`, so `?language=vi` and `?turns=voice` were lost; fixed with `toString()`;
    - a one-word answer after "Say it again" is no longer treated as talking over and dropped; pausing right as the customer starts to speak does not open an STT session;
    - relay: if the page stops sending audio for 10 s (tab frozen, microphone dead), close the session; ping every 20 s, with no pong release the session and the slot at the service; if the quota cannot be counted (database trouble), close with `unavailable` ("not available right now"), not "out of minutes for today".

## Consequences

- Each spoken sentence costs exactly its seconds of audio (60 minutes/day/account) and TTS characters; the two confirmation sentences are synthesized in advance on each entry into Voice (about 50 characters). `/ops` › Voice shows both amounts used today.
- The key needs the Text to Speech permission. Without it the conversation still runs, it just cannot speak: the voice row says "can't read aloud, the answer is in the chat".
- The voice the customer chose is stored in the browser (localStorage), not per account. Storing per account is for later.
- The Vietnamese accent of the default voices has not yet been checked by a listener. Scribe transcribing back found wrong diacritics in two words ("đổi" as "đối", "trang" as "trắng"). A human listener is needed before enabling widely.
- Echo cancellation on real external speakers is not measured: the real page and E2E run a fake microphone, with no speaker sound echoing back. Needs measuring on Chrome, Safari and the desktop app before enabling widely.
- The "yeah/yes" list and connective words are per-language data in code. Adding an interface language means adding words in `voice-turn-taking.mjs`.
- An "ok" said over Tracy exactly while it is reading the final question of an answer is treated as listening and dropped. The customer saying it again after Tracy stops works.
- A noisy room (background noise above the relay's speech threshold) makes each sentence wait up to 4 s more before sending.
- The STT can mishear even short sounds: on the stand "Ừ." ("Yeah.") once became "Cái gì?" ("What?"). That is a real question, so it is sent as a question.
- Items 13–16 have run on a real WordPress site freshly built on the local stand (17/10/2026, after the key was switched back on): editing a title by voice, a question card read with its options and answered by voice, the stop-work button, a correction while Tracy is working; images in `tasks/evidence/voice-barge-in-2026-10-16/`. Item 14's rule runs on dsh's host, so it takes effect only when the `tracy-dsh` image is rebuilt.
- Pressing Done/Enter during dictation now waits for the service to settle the sentence (usually 0.35–0.6 s) instead of closing immediately: the trade for not losing words.
- Words held after Stop live only in that Voice session: leaving Voice or reloading the page loses them (keeping them across reload is not promised yet).
- A spoken sentence longer than 1000 characters is compared on its first 1000 characters to attach the Voice rule (the `/view` door accepts at most 4 KB).
- Not done yet (council 17/10/2026, by priority):
  - P0: block consequential changes **at the execution layer** (the site-editing door requires a confirmation tied to the exact action and parameters); today it is only a rule in the prompt;
  - P1: per-turn trace (customer stops speaking, STT settles, send, TTS ready, first sound, done or cancelled; p50/p95); a real-human test set (200–300 sentences, Northern/Central/Southern accents, laptop and Bluetooth headset, noisy room, Safari, iPhone, Android, desktop app); a limit on concurrent STT sessions at the relay; `keyterms` taken from the site's own data; try Silero VAD in the browser, first in observe-only mode and only for turning the volume down; STT words still decide stopping the read-out (council 17/10/2026, VoiceStudio round);
  - P2: ending by voice ("tạm biệt" — "goodbye", "tắt Voice" — "turn off Voice" on their own); unify the agent's forms of address; approving a plan by voice (for now Voice takes the customer back to Chat to approve);
  - metadata travelling with the message for the Voice rule once dsh has a place for it (council 18/10/2026), replacing matching the message's text;
  - widget (#1425, being rewritten in #1426): an anonymous customer token is not refreshed after 72 h; 512 cookieless customers lock out new customers for 24 h; all anonymous customers share one quota.
- Won't do (council 17/10/2026, when looking at VoiceStudio — AGPL, code cannot be copied): reading clause by clause before the agent finishes writing (Tracy reads after the turn ends, so no gain); the Smart Turn turn-end model replacing the "và…" rule (it only cuts Tracy's wait, not measured on Vietnamese voices); lowering the threshold when there is sound but no words. Details: `tasks/evidence/voice-barge-in-2026-10-16/voicestudio-va-council-2026-10-17.md`.
