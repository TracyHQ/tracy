Build from my business makes a whole site from what is public about a business: its website, photos, reviews and social pages.

**Where:** Add site → Build it from my business.

**How:** type the business's name and city and pick it from the list, or paste its website or Google Maps link. Tracy reads what it finds, picks a template that suits the business and opens a chat where Tracy builds every page with the business's own name, contact details, opening hours, photos and reviews.

**Good to know:** reading the business takes a few minutes. Facts Tracy did not find are left out, never made up. There is a daily limit on how many sites one account can start this way. After the build, the site is yours to change in the chat like any other site.
