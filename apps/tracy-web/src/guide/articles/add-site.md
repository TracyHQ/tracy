Add site creates a new site or brings in an existing one.

**Where:** Add site in the sidebar or start screen.

**Create:** pick a platform (WordPress, Joomla or EmDash) and a design, then answer a few questions about the business.

**Import:** bring in an existing site. Tracy makes its own copy and your edits go to that copy, never back to your original server. To connect, Tracy may install or update its connector plugin on your existing site. For an imported WordPress or Joomla site, Sync from live later brings in what changed on the live site.

**Good to know:** Tracy works with sites built on WordPress, Joomla or EmDash. Shopify is not supported.
