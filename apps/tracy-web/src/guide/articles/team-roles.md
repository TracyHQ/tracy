Three roles, the same on every site:
- **Viewer:** sees the site, its changes and the team; can comment and ask Tracy questions, but Tracy will not change the site for them.
- **Editor:** changes the site with Tracy (pages, design, settings), publishes, backs up, restores and undoes; can invite Viewers or Editors.
- **Owner:** everything an Editor can, plus managing people (roles, removals, approving requests), the site's own domain, mail settings and deleting the site. A site can have several Owners.

**Where:** the site's Team tab (My Sites → "…" → Team).

**Invite someone:** Team → Invite member, pick Viewer or Editor. They get an email and an Inbox notice. Owners are made from the Team list, not by invitation.

**Good to know:**
- A Viewer who needs to edit can ask the Owners for edit access; an Owner answers with "Make them an Editor" or "Keep them a Viewer".
- A pending invitation can be cancelled or re-sent.
