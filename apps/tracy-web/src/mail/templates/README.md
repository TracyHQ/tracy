# Tracy access email templates

Source: the marketing team's reviewed handoff of the transactional access emails.

The HTML is copied into tracy-web so the deployed image is self-contained. Keep the public asset URLs and the approved layout. The renderer replaces the validated code, or the entire whitelist URL with the caller's escaped URL, including a signed token when configured. API-unavailable guidance replaces the membership opening paragraph. No real codes, addresses, credentials or tokens belong in these templates.

Both HTML and the existing plain-text fallback are sent through the configured Resend transport. The sender remains MAIL_FROM. Live Gmail/Outlook and mobile acceptance belongs to the later DEV rollout; this draft does not deploy or send email.

Since #753 the templates carry no words of their own: `{{lang}}` is the `<html lang>` tag, every `{{t:key}}` is that key of `src/lib/i18n/strings.js` in the reader's language (translations in `src/lib/i18n/locales/`), and `{{code}}`, `{{url}}`, `{{opening}}` are filled by `emails.js`. Changing a sentence means changing the dictionary and its seven translations, not this HTML.

Since 04/10/2026 every template ends with the same one-line footer, `footer.html`, filled by `brandFooter(t, manage)` in `../notification-mail.js` through the `{{brandFooter}}` marker: "Manage notifications · Terms · Privacy · tracy.ai" on notification letters, "Terms · Privacy · tracy.ai" on the sign-in and whitelist mails (they cannot be switched off) and on the site invitation (its reader may have no account, so no settings page to manage). No sign-off, tagline, "You received this…" line, trademark line, logo or company name. Change the footer in `footer.html` only; its words are the keys `mail.footerTerms` and `mail.footerPrivacy`, and the text part ends with the same two addresses (`brandFooterText(t)`). Since 05/10/2026 (spam hints C3-B) "tracy.ai" is plain text, not a link, and the logo is not a link either: a mail keeps only the links that do something (Accept/Review, Decline, Manage), because a mail from a new sender with many links scores as phishing. Since 06/10/2026 (after Brian) Terms and Privacy are the two exceptions, both on tracy.ai, the sending domain: a footer naming its terms reads as a real service. If inbox placement gets worse with them (measure as the spec's acceptance list says), they become plain text again.
