# Drawing prompt of the picture step

The drawer's fixed template (ADR 0043, #344). Code fills the `{{...}}` places from the site plan's words for one frame
(shows, mustInclude, avoid, mood, size) and nothing else, and for a cut-out frame (#346) the plain ground colour code
chose and will remove (cutout-ground.js), joins the `common` part with the part of the mode code chose,
adds the `style` part when the kit's demo picture of a cut-out frame is attached as a style reference, and adds the
`redraw` part on the one redraw after the looker rejected a drawing. Only the text under the `##` headings
is sent; this paragraph is not.

## common

Nothing in the picture may be readable: no letters, words, numbers, signs, logos, labels, watermarks or brand marks.
No frame, border, caption or collage. One single picture.
It must show: {{shows}}.
These must be clearly visible: {{mustInclude}}.
These must not appear: {{avoid}}.
Mood: {{mood}}.
The frame it fills on the website: {{size}}.

## scene

Make a realistic photograph, as a professional would take it for a small business website: natural light, true colours,
a real place, sharp where it matters. Keep the main subject whole inside the picture, with some space around it; never
cut a head, a face, hands or the tool in use at the edge. Faces, if any, small or turned away, and every hand and body
natural.

## object

Make a realistic product photograph of the one thing it must show, whole and centred, on a plain, quiet background that
suits the mood, with soft light and a little space on every side. No person in the picture.

## outpaint

The attached picture is a real photograph of the business, placed in the middle of a wider frame; the grey bands on
its left and right are empty. Extend the photograph into the grey bands so the whole frame is one continuous photograph
of the same place: the same light, colours, perspective and grain. Keep the middle photograph exactly as it is: do not
change, move, redraw or recolour anything in it. Add no new person and no new subject; continue only the surroundings
(walls, floor, background, the room or the street). No visible line, change of light or repeated pattern where the
photograph ends.

## cutout

Make a realistic studio photograph of one figure alone on a completely flat, plain background of one single colour:
{{ground}}. The background is exactly that colour everywhere: no gradient, no floor, no horizon, no shadow (not even a
small cast shadow under the feet), no reflection, no texture, no smoke or glow and no other thing on it. A cut-out has
no place: leave out any place "It must show" names (a court, a room, a street) and show only the figure and what it
holds. Nothing in the figure is that colour, not even a small part. The figure is the hero of the picture: it fills
about 90% of the picture's height, with only a small margin of background above it and at its left and right, and it may
stand on the bottom edge. Show the whole figure: every hand, arm, leg and foot, and everything the figure holds or wears
(gloves, a racket, a ball, a tool), is whole, inside the picture and sharp against the background. The figure looks like
an athlete of the sport or activity it must show, in fitted athletic sportswear for that sport. Do exactly what "It must
show" says the figure does and how it holds things (a racket under one arm stays under one arm), in a confident, active
stance typical of that sport, full of energy and attitude, never a stiff or passive catalogue pose. Light it like a
dramatic sports studio portrait: strong directional key light with deep shadows on the figure, and a rim light on its
edges is welcome, while the background stays one flat, even colour with no light spill, glow or gradient on it. Crisp
edges, no motion blur.

## style

The attached picture is the website's original picture for this frame, given ONLY as a style reference. Copy only its
framing, the scale of the figure (how much of the height it fills), the energy of the pose, the lighting and the
attitude. Never copy the person, the sport, the clothes, any equipment, the background graphics, shapes or colours of
the attached picture: the subject, the sport and what the figure holds come only from "It must show" above, and the
background is only the one plain colour named above.
