Tracy Cowork changes websites that run on WordPress, Joomla or EmDash when you ask for it in a chat.

1. **Sign in** at [cowork.tracy.ai](https://cowork.tracy.ai/) with your email address (Tracy mails
   you a sign-in code) or with your JoomlArt account. Anyone can sign up.
2. **Add a site.** Choose **Add site**, then **Create a new site** from a quickstart or a design
   template, or **Import existing site** to bring in a site that already runs.
3. **Ask for a change.** Type what you want in the chat, for example "change the phone number in the
   footer", and Tracy makes it on the real site. You can also pin a comment on the spot of the page
   that should change.
4. **Review it.** The **Site changes** tab lists every change, who made it and from where; open one to
   see the diff and undo it. A **Backup** keeps a point you can restore the whole site to.

## The desktop app

The same Tracy in its own window, for macOS, Windows and Linux: [download the app](/desktop/download/).

## Use Tracy from your AI app

Connect one website to an AI app through [Tracy MCP](/mcp/). Follow the setup guide for your client, then start with a read before making changes.

## Need help?

Type `/help` and your question in the chat to ask about Tracy and your site, or read
[Using Tracy](/using-tracy/).
