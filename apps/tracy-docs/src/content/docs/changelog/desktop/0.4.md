## Highlights
- Tracy Cowork now runs on Windows and Linux, as well as Mac. _(Desktop only)_
- Windows 10 and 11 (x64 and Arm): one installer per chip, no administrator rights needed. _(Desktop only)_
- Linux (x64 and arm64): an AppImage that updates itself, and a .deb package. _(Desktop only)_
- Mac: now needs macOS 13 or later. _(Desktop only)_
- Updates are verified with a Tracy signature before they install. _(Desktop only)_

## Changes
### New
- The Windows app, for Windows 10 and 11 on x64 and Arm, installs for your user account without administrator rights. _(Desktop only)_
- The Linux app, for x64 and arm64, comes as an AppImage that updates itself and as a .deb package. _(Desktop only)_
- Every update is checked against a Tracy signature before it installs, on every platform. _(Desktop only)_
### Changed
- The Mac app now needs macOS 13 or later. _(Desktop only)_
