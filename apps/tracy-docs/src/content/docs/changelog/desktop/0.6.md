## Changes
### New
- Updates download in the background and install when you quit the app, on Mac and Linux. On Windows the app still asks before it restarts. _(Desktop only)_
- Help & about shows what's new in the version you are running, with a link to the full changelog. After an update to a new minor version, one notification points you there. _(Desktop only)_
- A version that is no longer supported tells you so and offers the update, instead of failing quietly. _(Desktop only)_
- A new first-run setup: sign in, connect your AI account, pick your language and look, and check notifications, all in one place. _(Desktop only)_
- Sign in with your email through your browser, so you are signed in on the web too. _(Desktop only)_
- Tracy now shows when macOS blocks its notifications, and how to turn them on. _(Desktop only)_
- Documentation and the changelog live at docs.tracy.ai; the API reference moved to its own section there.
- Settings › AI accounts shows the same account cards as Getting started, with your default account and the fallbacks you can reorder.
- Tracy AI is one of your AI accounts: put it first, last, or anywhere in between.
- The Getting started screen can be closed with × and hidden from the account menu.
- The Getting started row in the account menu shows how many setup steps are done.
- If your access to a site is removed while its page is open, the chat says so, links to My Sites and keeps your draft.
- Site changes lists Tracy setup and API as sources of their own, and an undo made through the API gets its own row.
- Sign-in now tells you why it did not finish, and cancelling it in the app also cancels it in the browser. _(Desktop only)_
- Help answers and command results end with the same copy, rating, branch and time row as other answers, and the row stays under the latest answer until you send a new request.
- Review real customer campaigns in Ops with the original five groups, HTML email templates with images, customizable text fields and content approvals. Sending remains disabled.
### Fixed
- An update left waiting behind the "Restart now" dialog no longer stops the app from checking for newer versions. _(Desktop only)_
- The first-run setup no longer stays on "Looking for AI tools on this computer…" when your login shell is slow; after a few seconds it shows what it found and lets you refresh. _(Desktop only)_
- Signing out now ends your session on the server, so an old copy of it no longer signs you in.
- On a narrow screen, the side panel no longer opens over the message box by itself.
- On a phone, opening a site no longer covers the chat box with the site preview.
- On a phone, the Getting started dialog's close button stays inside the dialog.
- Images on EmDash sites created from Tracy SaaS, Tracy Business and Tracy Base load again.
