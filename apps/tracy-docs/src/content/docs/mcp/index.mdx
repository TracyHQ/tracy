Bring your website into the conversation. Tracy MCP connects your AI app to **one website you choose**, so you can find content, ask for an edit, and inspect the result without moving between admin screens.

## How it works

<Steps>

1. **Connect your AI app.** Sign in to Tracy and choose the website it can access.
2. **Ask for a change.** The app reads the current content and uses Tracy's tools to make a supported edit.
3. **Check the result.** Inspect the change receipt and read the content again. Request undo when the operation supports it.

</Steps>

Your AI app uses your account with that service. Your Tracy account determines which website it can reach. You do not share your Tracy password or a website administrator password in a chat.

## What you can do

- Find pages, published content, and supported records on the connected website.
- Read a record and its current revision before making a focused edit.
- Create, update, or trash records where the website supports that operation.
- Inspect change receipts and undo supported steps.

Tracy works with WordPress, Joomla, and EmDash. Available operations depend on the website's adapter and your permissions. This connection does not provide a shell, arbitrary file access, site provisioning, billing, or account administration.

<Aside type="note" title="One connection, one website">
The connection cannot expand your existing access. Choose a site where you have an accepted Editor or Owner role. See [Permissions & security](/mcp/permissions/) for access duration and revocation.
</Aside>

## Connection address

```text
https://cowork.tracy.ai/mcp
```

This address is for your AI app. Opening it in a browser can return **405 Method Not Allowed** because MCP clients connect using POST. The marketing website is `tracy.ai`; this documentation is at `docs.tracy.ai`.
