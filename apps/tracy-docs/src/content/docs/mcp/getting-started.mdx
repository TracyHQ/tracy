Connect once, then work on your website in a conversation. Start with a read before asking for your first change.

## What you need

- A Tracy account that can sign in at [cowork.tracy.ai](https://cowork.tracy.ai/).
- A website hosted or imported into Tracy, with an **accepted Editor or Owner invitation**.
- An AI app with the connection option described in its guide. Your app's account or workspace policy may limit custom connections.

Use a sample website for your first edit. You do not need an OpenAI developer organization just to use an available Tracy listing.

## Connect your website

```text wrap title="Tracy MCP server URL"
https://cowork.tracy.ai/mcp
```

<Steps>

1. **Add Tracy in your AI app.** Follow the guide for that app and start the connection.
2. **Sign in on cowork.tracy.ai.** Use the Tracy account that has access to the website.
3. **Check the connection request.** Confirm the app identity, choose the correct website, and read the requested access before connecting.
4. **Return to your AI app.** Enable Tracy for the conversation and run the read below.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Check the website before editing">
If your website is missing from the selection, check the Tracy account and accept your site invitation. Reconnecting cannot grant a stronger role.
</Aside>

## Try your first read

```text wrap title="Prompt"
Which Tracy website is connected? Show its name and the pages available there.
Do not change anything.
```

A successful connection returns the connected website and real page information through Tracy tools. A general explanation of MCP, a saved server URL, or an OAuth success message alone does not confirm a successful tool call.

## Make a small change

Once you have verified the website, follow [Working with your site](/mcp/working-with-your-site/) to read a specific record, change one field, and verify or undo it.
